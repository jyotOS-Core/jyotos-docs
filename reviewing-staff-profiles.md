## How Do I Access the Department Review Queue?

1. Open your browser and navigate to the StaffBridge login page.
2. Sign in using your official Google `@amrolicollege.ac.in` account.
3. Access the **Admin Portal** from the navigation menu.

## How Do I Review a Staff Profile?

### Step 1: Filter Pending Submissions

In the Admin Portal review queue, apply the status filter **Submitted** or **Correction Submitted** to view profiles waiting for department review.

### Step 2: Open a Staff Record

Click on a staff member's row or Staff Reference ID to open their complete profile review sheet.

### Step 3: Evaluate Personal & Contact Information

Verify that the staff member's **Full Name** is formatted correctly (e.g. `Dr. Hiral A. Patel` or `Ms. Priyanka Mitesh Patel`), the **Date of Birth** matches official records, and the **Email Address** ends strictly with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.

### Step 4: Verify Employment & Department Assignment

Confirm that the assigned **Primary Campus** (**Amroli Campus** or **Chaprabhatha Campus**), **Primary Department**, **Staff Category**, **Official Designation**, and **Date of Joining** match institutional appointment records.

### Step 5: Check Academic Qualifications

Cross-check selected qualifications (e.g., M.A., Ph.D., NET, GSET) against official college appointment files.

### Step 6: Inspect Identification Media

Inspect the uploaded identification media:

* **Passport Photo:** Ensure the photo is clear, front-facing, and taken against a light background.
* **Signature Image:** Ensure signature lines are clear, continuous, and legible.
