Welcome to the **Getting Started Guide** for StaffBridge. This guide provides a high-level preparation checklist and overview of the staff onboarding registration journey.

***

## Pre-Registration Checklist

Before beginning your registration, ensure you have the following information and documents ready:

* **Official Email Address:** Your active `@amrolicollege.ac.in` Google account.
* **Personal & Emergency Contact Details:** Full legal name, date of birth, blood group, mobile number, and emergency contact details.
* **Employment Information:** Official designation, primary campus, primary department, staff category, and date of joining.
* **Academic Qualifications:** List of completed degrees (e.g., M.A., Ph.D., NET, GSET).
* **Passport Photo:** A clear, front-facing portrait photo file (JPEG or PNG, under 2 MB).
* **Signature Image:** A clear scanned signature file or ability to draw your digital signature on screen (under 1 MB).

***

## Onboarding Registration Interface

The onboarding registration form is organized into logical sections to guide you through entering your details smoothly.

***

## The 6-Step Onboarding Journey

```
1. Personal Information ──► 2. Professional Info ──► 3. Campus & Department ──► 4. Photo & Signature ──► 5. Review ──► 6. Receipt
```

1. **Step 1: Access Onboarding:** Navigate to `/staff/onboarding` on your desktop or mobile browser.
2. **Step 2: Fill Personal & Employment Info:** Enter your personal, contact, employment, and qualification details.
3. **Step 3: Map Campus & Department:** Select your Primary Campus (**Amroli Campus** or **Chaprabhatha Campus**) and Primary Department.
4. **Step 4: Upload Photo & Signature:** Upload your passport photo and signature image or draw your digital signature.
5. **Step 5: Review & Submit:** Confirm your entries and click **Submit Staff Information**.
6. **Step 6: Download Receipt:** Save your PDF submission receipt containing your Staff Reference ID.
