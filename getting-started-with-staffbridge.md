Welcome to StaffBridge! This guide provides everything you need to know to prepare your information, complete your staff onboarding, and track your profile verification.

***

## Before You Begin Checklist

Before starting your onboarding registration, ensure you have the following information and items ready:

* [ ] **Official Institutional Email:** Your official college email address ending with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.
* [ ] **Personal & Emergency Contact Details:** Your 10-digit mobile number, alternate mobile number (optional), and emergency contact name/number.
* [ ] **Academic & Professional Information:** Date of joining, staff category, official designation, area of expertise, and list of educational qualifications.
* [ ] **Identification Media & Blood Group:** A clear passport-style portrait photo, your signature (drawn on canvas or uploaded as an image), and your blood group.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Open Onboarding Portal** Initial staff onboarding registration does **not** require prior account login. You can access the registration form directly at `/staff/onboarding`.
{% endhint %}

***

## Official Institutional Email Requirement

To maintain institutional data governance and security, StaffBridge strictly requires an official college email address:

$$\text{username}@\text{amrolicollege.ac.in}$$

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Official Email Only** Submissions using personal email addresses (such as Gmail, Yahoo, or Outlook) will be rejected by the validation system. Always use your official `@amrolicollege.ac.in` address.
{% endhint %}

***

## Understanding the Onboarding Journey

Your registration and profile verification follow a simple six-step lifecycle:

```
Open StaffBridge ──► Complete Onboarding ──► Receive Reference ID ──► Profile Review ──► Correction if Required ──► Final Verification
```

### 1. Access the Onboarding Portal

Open the public registration form at `/staff/onboarding`.

### 2. Complete Onboarding Details

Select your primary campus (**Amroli Campus** or **Chaprabhatha Campus**) and primary department. Enter your personal details, designation, qualifications, blood group, profile photo, signature, and media consent.

### 3. Receive Staff Reference ID

Upon successful submission, the system generates a unique **Staff Reference ID** (formatted as `STAFF-2026-XXXX`). Save this reference number for your records. You can also download an official PDF receipt directly from the confirmation screen.

### 4. Department & Administrative Review

Your submitted record enters the verification queue. Your Department Coordinator and System Administrator will review your submitted details and identification media.

### 5. Respond to Corrections (If Flagged)

If any field requires clarification (such as formatting or a missing detail), an administrator will flag the field. You will receive an alert to log into your dashboard (`/staff/dashboard`) using your official Google `@amrolicollege.ac.in` account to submit updated information.

### 6. Receive Final Verification

Once fully approved by administration, your profile receives final verification.

***

## Overview of Profile Statuses

As your profile moves through review, it will display one of the following statuses on your dashboard:

* **Submitted:** Your registration has been received and is waiting for review.
* **Coordinator Verified:** Your profile has been reviewed and verified by your Department Coordinator.
* **Needs Correction:** An administrator or coordinator has requested updated information for specific fields.
* **Correction Submitted:** You have submitted updated details and your profile is awaiting final review.
* **Admin Verified:** Your profile is fully verified and approved by administration.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Need Assistance?** If you encounter any issues during onboarding, contact your Department Coordinator or System Administrator.
{% endhint %}
