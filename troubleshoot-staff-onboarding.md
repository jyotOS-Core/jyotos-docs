This guide provides troubleshooting solutions for issues encountered while filling out or submitting the staff onboarding registration form.

***

## Common Form Issues & Solutions

| What You See                      | Likely Cause                                  | What To Do                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Form Won't Submit**             | Missing mandatory field or invalid format     | Check for red highlight borders. Ensure mobile numbers contain 10 digits and address includes pincode.               |
| **Email Field Rejected**          | Personal email entered                        | Enter your official college email ending strictly with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.                                       |
| **Department List Empty**         | Campus not selected                           | Select your Primary Campus (**Amroli Campus** or **Chaprabhatha Campus**) first to populate the department dropdown. |
| **"Duplicate Submission" Notice** | Profile already registered under email/mobile | Do not resubmit. Sign in to your dashboard to view your existing profile status.                                     |

***

## Verified Error Messages

* **Message:** _"Please enter a valid 10-digit mobile number."_\
  _&#x57;hat It Means:_ Mobile number contains spaces, letters, or fewer/more than 10 digits.\
  _&#x57;hat You Should Do:_ Enter exactly 10 numerical digits without spaces or country codes.
* **Message:** _"Duplicate registration detected."_\
  _&#x57;hat It Means:_ An onboarding profile already exists for your email address or mobile number.\
  _&#x57;hat You Should Do:_ Log into your dashboard to view your registered profile or contact your System Administrator if registered in error.

***

## Still Need Help?

If form validation fails repeatedly, take a screenshot of the error message and contact your assigned Department Coordinator.
