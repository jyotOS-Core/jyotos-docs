***

## Dashboard Issues Troubleshooting

| What You Experience                  | Likely Cause                                  | What To Do                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ID Card Preview Blank**            | Photo or signature media missing or in review | Complete media upload. ID preview renders once photo and signature are submitted and verified. |
| **Dashboard Shows Wrong Department** | Initial department selection error            | Contact System Administrator to update your primary department assignment.                     |
| **Push Notifications Not Received**  | Browser push permissions blocked              | Click **Enable Push Notifications** on dashboard and allow browser notification prompts.       |
