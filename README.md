Welcome to the **JyotOS Help Center** — the official documentation, user guide, and support portal for all applications across the JyotOS institutional management ecosystem.

JyotOS provides an integrated Institution Management System (IMS) designed to support academic administration, staff governance, event operations, and campus workflows. This portal provides clear, step-by-step guidance for every role, from first-time sign-in to advanced system administration.

***

## Who This Help Center is For

* **Institutional Faculty & Staff Members:** Learn how to sign in once with your official college Google account, use the Central app launcher, update your profile, and complete daily tasks.
* **Department Coordinators & Heads of Department:** Manage department queues, review and verify staff submissions, and coordinate campus events.
* **Institutional Leadership:** Oversee platform adoption, review operational governance, and maintain institutional visibility.
* **System Administrators:** Manage application registries, user access audiences, platform roles, and security audit trails.
* **Partner Institutions:** Understand how JyotOS ecosystem applications integrate and review onboarding guidelines.

## How to Get Support

Finding answers in the Help Center is simple:

* **Search Documentation:** Press **Ctrl + K** (or **Cmd + K** on macOS) at any time to search topics, guides, or error codes.
* **Follow Step-by-Step Guides:** Access structured walkthroughs with clear checklists and status definitions.
* **Troubleshoot Issues:** Review self-service guides in [Central Troubleshooting](products/central/troubleshooting.md) and the [StaffBridge Troubleshooting Center](products/staffbridge/troubleshooting/README.md).
* **Submit Feedback:** Share recommendations or report unexpected behavior via the [Feedback & Suggestions](feedback-and-suggestions.md) page.
