{% hint style="info" %}

* **Audience:** Faculty, Staff, Coordinators, Principals, and System Administrators
* **Applies To:** JyotOS Ecosystem
* **Last Updated:** November 2026
{% endhint %}

Welcome to the **JyotOS Help Center** — the official documentation, user guide, and support portal for all applications across the JyotOS institutional management ecosystem.

JyotOS provides an integrated Institution Management System (IMS) designed to support academic administration, staff governance, event operations, and campus workflows. This portal provides clear, step-by-step guidance for every role, from first-time sign-in to advanced system administration.

---

## Who This Help Center is For

* **Faculty & Staff Members:** Learn how to sign in once with your official college Google account (`@amrolicollege.ac.in`), use the Central app launcher, update your profile, and complete daily tasks.
* **Department Coordinators & Heads of Department:** Manage department queues, review and verify staff submissions, and coordinate campus events.
* **Institutional Leadership:** Oversee platform adoption, review operational governance, and maintain institutional visibility across departments.
* **System Administrators:** Manage application registries, user access audiences, platform roles, and administrative audit logs.
* **Partner Institutions:** Understand how JyotOS ecosystem applications integrate and review onboarding guidelines.

## How to Get Support

* **Search Documentation:** Press **Ctrl + K** (or **Cmd + K** on macOS) at any time to search topics or terms.
* **Department Questions:** Contact your **Department Coordinator** or **Head of Department (HOD)**.
* **Technical Assistance:** Contact the **College IT Cell** or your **System Administrator**.
