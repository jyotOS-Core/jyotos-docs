This guide helps Department Coordinators and System Administrators resolve management-side queue, scope, and reporting issues.

***

## Management Troubleshooting Decision Table

| What You Experience                         | Likely Cause                                                    | What To Do                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Staff Record Missing in Queue**           | Record mapped to different primary department                   | Verify staff member's selected primary department. Coordinators see assigned department records only.    |
| **Cannot Grant Final Verification**         | Profile status is not yet Coordinator Verified                  | Coordinator must review and approve profile to **Coordinator Verified** before final Admin Verification. |
| **CSV Report Export Empty**                 | Filters too restrictive                                         | Clear department/status filters in **Staff Reports & Verification Roster** page before clicking Export.  |
| **Staff Member Inactive in Connected Apps** | Profile is Admin Verified, but Active Status is set to Inactive | Open profile in Admin Portal, toggle Active Status to **Active**, and save.                              |

***

## Support Escalation

* **Coordinators:** Escalate system configuration or role scope issues to the System Administrator.
* **Administrators:** Escalate unresolved core system issues to the designated StaffBridge support representative.
