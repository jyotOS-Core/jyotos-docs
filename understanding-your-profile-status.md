This guide explains each status code displayed on your StaffBridge dashboard, what it indicates about your registration, and what action you should take.

***

## Profile Review Lifecycle

Your registration moves through a progressive review lifecycle:

```
Submitted ──► Coordinator Verified ──► Needs Correction ──► Correction Submitted ──► Admin Verified
```

### 1. Submitted

* **Meaning:** Your onboarding registration has been received successfully and entered into the institutional queue.
* **Review Level:** Awaiting initial review by your Department Coordinator or System Administrator.
* **Staff Action Required:** None. Your submission is being processed.

### 2. Coordinator Verified

* **Meaning:** Your assigned Department Coordinator has reviewed and verified your department mapping, designation, and academic qualifications.
* **Review Level:** Department review complete. Awaiting final administrative approval.
* **Staff Action Required:** None. Your profile is advancing to final administrative review.

### 3. Needs Correction

* **Meaning:** An administrator or coordinator has reviewed your profile and flagged one or more specific fields that require clarification or updated information.
* **Review Level:** Action needed from staff member.
* **Staff Action Required:** Sign in to your dashboard. View the **Pending Correction Fields** section, update the flagged details, and submit your update.

### 4. Correction Submitted

* **Meaning:** You have submitted updated details in response to a correction request.
* **Review Level:** Awaiting administrative re-evaluation.
* **Staff Action Required:** None. An administrator will review your resubmitted information.

### 5. Admin Verified

* **Meaning:** Your profile has received final approval from the System Administrator or Principal.
* **Review Level:** Complete.
* **Staff Action Required:** None. Your profile is fully verified across institutional systems.

***

## Additional Status Codes

### Duplicate

* **Meaning:** The system detected a duplicate registration under your email or mobile number.
* **Staff Action Required:** If you registered twice by mistake, no action is needed. If marked in error, contact your System Administrator.

### Rejected

* **Meaning:** The registration could not be processed by college administration.
* **Staff Action Required:** Contact your Department Coordinator or System Administrator for guidance on re-registering.
