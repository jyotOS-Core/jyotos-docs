This guide explains the preconditions and procedure for Department Coordinators to grant coordinator-level verification to a staff record.

***

## Preconditions for Department Verification

Before approving a profile, ensure all of the following conditions are satisfied:

* [ ] **Field Accuracy:** All personal, contact, employment, and academic details have been verified against college records.
* [ ] **Email Validity:** Official college email address ending with `@amrolicollege.ac.in` is confirmed.
* [ ] **Media Standards:** Passport photo and signature image meet visual quality standards.
* [ ] **Zero Correction Flags:** All flagged fields have been resolved and resubmitted satisfactorily.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Verification Policy** Do **not** grant verification to an incomplete profile or a profile with pending correction flags. Verification should only be granted after thorough review.
{% endhint %}

***

## How Do I Verify a Staff Profile?

1. Open the staff profile in the Admin Portal.
2. Perform a final review of all fields and media.
3. Click **Approve / Grant Verification**.
4. The system records your coordinator ID and timestamp, and updates the profile status to **Coordinator Verified**.

***

## Profile Status Outcome & Next Steps

* **New Status:** **Coordinator Verified**.
* **Next Lifecycle Step:** The profile advances automatically to the System Administrator queue for final administrative verification and ecosystem access enablement.
