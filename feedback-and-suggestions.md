Your feedback is essential in shaping the **JyotOS Help Center** into a world-class documentation portal.

We welcome suggestions, corrections, and inquiries from internal faculty, department coordinators, and administrative leadership evaluating this Internal Preview release.

***

## Types of Feedback We Invite

* **Documentation Clarity:** Suggestions to clarify instructions or simplify terminology.
* **Missing Information:** Additional topics, fields, or scenarios that require documentation.
* **Errors & Typos:** Reporting broken links, typographical errors, or formatting issues.
* **Navigation & Usability:** Feedback on searchability, sidebar organization, or guide readability.
* **Feature & Workflow Requests:** Proposing new user guides or help resources.

***

## How to Submit Feedback

{% hint style="info" %}
**Feedback Collection Mechanism** Centralized digital feedback forms and direct helpdesk email integration will be finalized in the upcoming release. For this Internal Preview phase, please share your feedback directly with the **System Administrator** or **JyotOS Development Team**.
{% endhint %}
