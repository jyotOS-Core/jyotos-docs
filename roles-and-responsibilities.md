This guide provides a comprehensive governance matrix defining roles, permissions, and operational boundaries across StaffBridge.

***

## Institutional Roles Governance Matrix

| Role                       | Access Level                 | Primary Responsibilities                                                                               | Review & Operational Boundary |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| **Staff Member**           | Self-Service Portal          | Submits onboarding registration, uploads media, tracks status, responds to correction flags            | Personal Profile Only         |
| **Department Coordinator** | Admin Portal                 | Reviews department submissions, flags field corrections, approves profiles to **Coordinator Verified** | Assigned Department Personnel |
| **College Principal**      | Admin Portal & Staff Reports | Executive oversight, college-wide roster monitoring, final verification review                         | Institution-Wide (All Units)  |
| **System Administrator**   | Admin Portal & Staff Reports | System access management, final **Admin Verified** approval, Active Status control, CSV exports        | Institution-Wide (All Units)  |

***

## Operational Escalation Path

* **Unresolved Correction Requests:** Staff members with questions regarding reviewer remarks should contact their assigned Department Coordinator.
* **Department Re-assignment or Locked Fields:** Coordinators needing to update locked department mappings or reset submissions should escalate to the System Administrator.
* **Ecosystem Access Inquiries:** Issues regarding connected application eligibility should be directed to the System Administrator.
