This guide helps resolve issues when responding to field-level correction requests from department coordinators or administrators.

***

## Common Correction Issues & Fixes

| What You Experience                 | Likely Cause                                    | What To Do                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Reviewer Remark Unclear**         | Remark instructions need clarification          | Contact your Department Coordinator directly for guidance before resubmitting.       |
| **Field Remains Locked**            | Immutable field flagged for correction          | Contact your System Administrator to reset the locked field.                         |
| **"Correction Submitted" for Days** | Resubmission awaiting coordinator re-evaluation | Allow 1–2 business days for re-evaluation. Contact Department Coordinator if urgent. |
| **Multiple Fields Flagged**         | Errors present across several sections          | Update all flagged fields on your dashboard before clicking **Submit Update**.       |

***

## Escalation Path for Correction Issues

```
Read Reviewer Remark ──► Update Fields on Dashboard ──► Contact Coordinator (If Unclear) ──► Contact System Administrator
```
