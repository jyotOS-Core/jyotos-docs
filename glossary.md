# Glossary

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** All JyotOS Users
* **Applies To:** Terminology across JyotOS documentation and on-screen interfaces
{% endhint %}

This glossary defines common terms, on-screen labels, and concepts used throughout the JyotOS platform and documentation.

---

### Access Audience
A predefined group or departmental criteria configured in JyotOS Central to provision application access collectively to multiple staff members.

### Admin Verified
The final verification status in StaffBridge, performed by a system administrator, confirming official institutional credentials and establishing a Ready profile.

### Amroli Campus
The primary institutional campus location for Amroli College academic departments and administrative offices.

### Audit Log
A chronological administrative record in JyotOS Central tracking operational actions, catalog updates, access grants, and role assignments.

### Central (JyotOS Central)
The central web launcher and access management hub (`https://jyotos.vercel.app`) where staff discover and open authorized campus applications.

### Chaprabhatha Campus
The second institutional campus facility included in campus selection, venue allocations, and event hosting.

### ClassPulse Pro
The academic lecture reconciliation and teaching portal for monitoring faculty lecture delivery, timetable allocations, and teaching diaries.

### Coordinator (Department Coordinator)
A designated departmental faculty leader responsible for reviewing staff onboarding submissions and proposing departmental events.

### Coordinator Verified
The first-tier verification status in StaffBridge indicating that a department coordinator has reviewed and approved a staff member's submission.

### Delivery Benchmark (85%)
The institutional performance target within ClassPulse Pro representing the expected proportion of conducted lectures relative to scheduled timetable sessions.

### EventOS
The campus event management application used to propose, approve, publish, and register for institutional seminars, workshops, and programs.

### Head of Department (HOD)
An academic department executive responsible for supervising departmental faculty rosters, teaching workloads, and curriculum delivery.

### JyotOS
The integrated digital campus Institution Management System (IMS) connecting identity, academic tools, event coordination, and administrative operations.

### Lecture Reconciler
An analytical tool in ClassPulse Pro comparing contracted weekly teaching hours against conducted teacher diary entries to identify lecture delivery variances.

### Pilot Staff
A designated group of faculty members and coordinators granted early access to validate application workflows and provide feedback prior to campus-wide rollout.

### Principal
The executive head of the college responsible for campus-wide governance, final event approvals, and cross-department academic oversight.

### Ready Profile
A staff profile in StaffBridge that has successfully completed both Coordinator Review and Admin Verification with Active status, enabling access across connected applications.

### Request Access
A self-service button on locked application cards in JyotOS Central allowing staff members to submit an access request to system administrators.

### Sign in Once (Single Sign-On)
The ability to access connected JyotOS applications using your official college Google account (`@amrolicollege.ac.in`) without maintaining separate usernames or passwords.

### Staff Reference Number (Staff ID)
A unique institutional identifier assigned to a staff member upon successful administrator verification in StaffBridge.

### StaffBridge
The central identity and staff onboarding platform that manages verified personnel profiles, campus mappings, and digital credential directories.

### Subject Division Matrix
A master scheduling view in ClassPulse Pro showing assigned courses, classroom divisions, and designated instructors.

### System Administrator
An IT specialist or member of the College IT Cell responsible for managing user access, Central application registries, and system configurations.

### Teaching Diary
A digital register within ClassPulse Pro where faculty members record chronological details of conducted lectures, classroom divisions, and syllabus topics taught.
