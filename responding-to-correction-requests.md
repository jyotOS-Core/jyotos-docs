This guide explains how to view, update, and resubmit information if an administrator or department coordinator requests a correction on your staff profile.

***

## How You Are Notified

If your profile status changes to **Needs Correction**, you will be notified via:

1. **Email Notification:** An automated email listing the flagged fields and reviewer instructions.
2. **Dashboard Alert:** A prominent **Pending Correction Fields** banner displayed when you log into `/staff/dashboard`.

***

## How to Respond to a Correction Request

Follow these simple steps to update your profile:

### Step 1: Log Into Your Dashboard

Navigate to `/login` and sign in using your official Google `@amrolicollege.ac.in` account.

### Step 2: Locate Pending Corrections

On your dashboard, locate the **Pending Correction Fields** section. Each flagged field displays:

* The field name requiring update.
* The reviewer's comment or instructions (e.g., _"Please update address with 6-digit pincode"_).

### Step 3: Enter Corrected Information

Input your updated details in the provided form field:

* **Text / Select Fields:** Enter text or choose a new option from the dropdown.
* **Qualification Checklist:** Update your qualification selection.
* **Media Re-upload:** Capture or upload a fresh passport photo or signature image if requested.

### Step 4: Add Optional Remarks

Enter an optional remark for the reviewer explaining the change made.

### Step 5: Submit Update

Click **Submit Update**. Your profile status automatically changes to **Correction Submitted**.

***

## What Happens Afterwards?

* An administrator will review your resubmitted information alongside the original entries.
* Upon approval, the updated details are automatically written to your official staff record.
* Once all corrections are approved, your profile advances to **Admin Verified**.

***

## Helpful Tips & Common Mistakes

* **Double-Check Formatting:** Ensure mobile numbers contain 10 digits and addresses include pincodes.
* **Check Reviewer Remarks:** Read the reviewer's instructions carefully before submitting updates.
* **Official Email Only:** If updating contact details, ensure email addresses end with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.

***

## When to Contact Your Administrator

Contact your System Administrator or Department Coordinator if:

* You cannot modify a locked profile field that requires correction.
* You need assistance re-uploading identification media.
* You have questions about a reviewer's comment.
