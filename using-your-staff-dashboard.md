## Key Dashboard Features

### 1. Profile Overview Card

Displays your key registration details:

* **Staff Reference ID:** Your unique institutional code (e.g. `STAFF-2026-0086`).
* **Profile Status Badge:** Color-coded status badge showing current review progress.
* **Primary Department & Campus:** Your assigned primary academic/admin unit and campus location.
* **Designation & Category:** Official job title and employment classification.

***

### 2. Live Digital ID Card Preview

Renders a real-time preview of your institutional digital staff ID card:

* Passport portrait photograph.
* Scanned or drawn signature image.
* Scannable QR verification code.
* Blood group and emergency contact details.
* Date of joining and campus department assignment.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Digital ID Card Preview** Your ID card preview renders automatically once your photo and signature are submitted and verified.
{% endhint %}

***

### 3. Pending Correction Section

If an administrator flags a field for correction, a dedicated action section appears on your dashboard allowing you to enter corrected details and submit updates directly.

***

### 4. Push Notification Controls

Allows you to opt-in or opt-out of browser push notifications to receive real-time alerts on your mobile device or desktop when your profile status is updated.

***
