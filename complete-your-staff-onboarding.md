This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough to help new and existing staff members complete their onboarding registration in StaffBridge.

***

## Who Should Use This Guide

This guide is designed for all institutional personnel joining Amroli College, including:

* **Teaching Faculty:** Permanent, adhoc, and visiting faculty members across all departments.
* **Administrative Staff:** Permanent and adhoc administrative and office personnel.
* **Library Staff:** Permanent and adhoc library professionals and attendants.
* **Support & Technical Staff:** Support personnel and laboratory technicians across campuses.

***

## Before You Begin Checklist

Before opening the registration form, ensure you have the following information and items ready:

* [ ] **Official Institutional Email:** Your official college email ending with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.
* [ ] **Contact Details:** Your 10-digit primary mobile number and an optional alternate mobile number.
* [ ] **Emergency Contact:** Emergency contact person's name and their 10-digit mobile number.
* [ ] **Employment & Academic Details:** Date of joining, official designation, staff category, area of expertise, and list of educational qualifications.
* [ ] **Identification Media & Blood Group:** A clear passport-style portrait photo, your signature image (drawn on canvas or uploaded), and your blood group.

{% hint style="info" %}
**No Prior Login Required** Initial staff onboarding registration does **not** require prior account sign-in. You can open the registration form directly.
{% endhint %}

***

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Access StaffBridge

Open your web browser and navigate to the public onboarding registration portal at `/staff/onboarding`.

### Step 2: Select Your Campus

Select your primary campus location (**Amroli Campus** or **Chaprabhatha Campus**).

### Step 3: Select Your Department

Choose your **Primary Department** from the dropdown menu (filtered automatically by your campus selection). If you teach or work across multiple departments, check any additional departments under the **Additional Departments** list.

### Step 4: Complete Personal Information

Enter your **Full Name**, **Gender**, **Date of Birth**, 10-digit **Mobile Number**, optional **Alternate Mobile Number**, and official **Email Address** ending with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`. Enter your **Residential Address**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Email Validation** Personal email addresses (e.g., Gmail, Yahoo) are not accepted. Ensure you enter your official `@amrolicollege.ac.in` email address.
{% endhint %}

### Step 5: Provide Employment Information

Select your **Staff Category** (e.g., Permanent Teaching Staff, Adhoc Administrative Staff) and official **Designation**. Enter your **Date of Joining**, **Area of Expertise**, **Emergency Contact Name**, and **Emergency Contact Number**.

### Step 6: Enter Academic Qualifications

Check all applicable educational qualifications from the list (e.g., B.A., M.A., Ph.D., NET, GSET). If you hold a degree not listed, select **Other** and specify the degree name in the text field.

### Step 7: Upload Passport Photo

Capture a clear front-facing portrait photo using your device camera or upload a clear image file from your computer.

### Step 8: Upload Signature Image

Draw your signature clearly on the digital canvas using a mouse or touch screen, or upload a scanned signature image file.

### Step 9: Review Your Information

Review all entered details carefully for accuracy. Read the media consent statement and check the consent box to authorize institutional identification usage.

### Step 10: Submit Registration

Click **Submit Onboarding Details**. The system validates your entries and processes your submission.

***

## After Submission

Upon successful registration, the confirmation screen will display:

1. **Staff Reference ID:** A unique institutional reference code formatted as `STAFF-2026-XXXX`. Save this reference number for your records.
2. **Confirmation Timestamp:** The exact date and time your registration was received.
3. **Download PDF Receipt:** Click the **Download PDF Receipt** button to generate an official PDF confirmation document for your personal records.

***

## What Happens Next?

Your registration is automatically submitted to the institutional review queue:

* **Department Review:** Your Department Coordinator will review your submitted department and academic credentials.
* **Administrative Review:** System Administrators or the Principal will perform final verification.
* **Tracking:** You can log into your dashboard at `/staff/dashboard` using your official Google `@amrolicollege.ac.in` account at any time to track your status.
