This guide provides a detailed, step-by-step walkthrough to fill out your onboarding form, upload identification media, and complete registration.

***

## Step-by-Step Registration Procedure

### Step 1: Personal & Contact Information

Enter your Full Name, Gender, Date of Birth, 10-digit Mobile Number, Alternate Mobile Number, and official `@amrolicollege.ac.in` email address. Specify your Emergency Contact Name and Emergency Contact Number.

### Step 2: Employment & Professional Details

Select your **Staff Category** (Teaching, Non-Teaching, Administrative, Support) and **Official Designation**. Enter your **Date of Joining** and select your academic qualifications.

### Step 3: Campus & Department Mapping

Select your **Primary Campus** (**Amroli Campus** or **Chaprabhatha Campus**) to dynamically load the corresponding department list. Select your **Primary Department** and check any additional department assignments.

### Step 4: Photo & Signature Upload

Select your Blood Group. Upload a front-facing passport portrait and upload or draw your digital signature. Check the mandatory consent checkbox.

### Step 5: Review & Submit Registration

Review your selection summary in the final section before submitting:

1. Confirm all summary details match your appointment.
2. Check the confirmation box: _"I confirm that the above information is correct."_
3. Click **Submit Staff Information**.

***

## Post-Submission Confirmation

Upon successful submission, the system generates your unique **Staff Reference ID** (formatted as `SB-2026-XXXX`). Click **Download PDF Receipt** to save a copy for your records.
