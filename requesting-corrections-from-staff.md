This guide explains when and how to flag field-level errors on a staff profile and how to write clear, actionable reviewer remarks.

***

## When Should I Request a Correction?

Request a field correction whenever submitted information is inaccurate, incomplete, or fails quality standards:

* **Incomplete Addresses:** Address missing 6-digit pincode or landmark details.
* **Formatting Errors:** Name entered in lowercase or missing official titles.
* **Media Quality Issues:** Blurry portrait photo, dark background, or cropped signature.
* **Category Mismatch:** Incorrect staff category or designation selected.

***

## Writing Effective Reviewer Remarks

Reviewer remarks are displayed directly to the staff member on their dashboard. Remarks must be **polite**, **specific**, and **instructional**.

### Reviewer Remark Guidance

| Field Issue         | ❌ Poor Remark (Vague / Unhelpful) | 🟢 Good Remark (Actionable & Polite)                                                        |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Address Pincode** | _"Wrong address."_                | _"Please enter your complete residential address including your 6-digit pincode."_          |
| **Signature Media** | _"Fix signature."_                | _"Please upload a clear signature image drawn with dark ink on plain white paper."_         |
| **Qualification**   | _"Check degrees."_                | _"Please select M.Sc. and specify your specialization subject under Other Qualifications."_ |
| **Designation**     | _"Incorrect role."_               | _"Please select Assistant Professor as your official appointment designation."_             |

1. Open the staff profile sheet in the Admin Portal.
2. Check the box next to each specific field requiring correction.
3. Enter actionable reviewer remarks in the comment box.
4. Click **Request Correction**.
5. Staff status automatically transitions to **Needs Correction**.

***

## How Do I Review Resubmissions?

* When the staff member resubmits updated information, their status changes to **Correction Submitted**.
* Open the profile in the Admin Portal to review the updated entries alongside your original remarks.
* If satisfied, approve the profile to **Coordinator Verified**.
