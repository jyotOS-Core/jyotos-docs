## General Platform Questions

### What is JyotOS?
JyotOS is the integrated institutional management system for Amroli Campus and Chaprabhatha Campus. It connects identity, academic administration, event coordination, and operations under a unified digital environment.

### What applications are currently active in JyotOS?
The four live applications in active use are:
* **JyotOS Central:** The central launcher and access control hub.
* **StaffBridge:** The official staff onboarding and digital credential directory.
* **EventOS:** Campus event proposal, approval, and participant registration.
* **ClassPulse Pro:** Faculty timetable tracking and lecture reconciliation.

### How does sign-in work across JyotOS?
Staff members sign in once using their official college Google account ending in `@amrolicollege.ac.in`. When your profile is verified in StaffBridge and reaches Ready status, your identity is recognized across connected platform applications without separate passwords.

### Which email account must I use?
All JyotOS applications strictly require your institutional Google account ending in `@amrolicollege.ac.in`. Personal email accounts (such as `@gmail.com` or `@yahoo.com`) are not accepted.

## Roles and Access

### How are user permissions determined?
Access to applications is provisioned through JyotOS Central by administrators. Inside each application, roles (such as Staff, Department Coordinator, Principal, or Administrator) determine specific operational capabilities and department scoping.

### What should I do if an application shows a lock icon?
In JyotOS Central, click the **Request Access** button on the locked application card. Your request will be forwarded to the System Administrator for review and approval.

### Who should I contact if my profile is not verified?
First-tier verification is conducted by your Department Coordinator. If there are delays, check with your coordinator or reach out to the College IT Cell.
