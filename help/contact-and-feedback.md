# Contact & Feedback

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** All Institutional Faculty, Staff, and Administrators
* **Applies To:** Help Center Feedback & Technical Assistance
* **Channels:** Department Leadership and College IT Cell
{% endhint %}

Your feedback helps refine the JyotOS platform and documentation for faculty and staff across Amroli Campus and Chaprabhatha Campus.

---

## Where to Get Help

### 1. Departmental & Academic Inquiries
For questions regarding profile verification, department assignment, or academic course allocations:
* Contact your **Department Coordinator** or **Head of Department (HOD)**.

### 2. Technical Support & System Access
For login difficulties, application access provisioning, or system error reports:
* Contact the **College IT Cell** or your **System Administrator**.

---

## Submitting Documentation Feedback

We welcome suggestions to improve guide clarity, correct typographical errors, or expand workflow explanations:

* **Inaccurate or Outdated Steps:** If a button, label, or sequence in the documentation differs from what you see on screen.
* **Missing Scenarios:** Suggestions for additional workflows or common questions that should be included.
* **Clarity & Formatting:** Suggestions to improve readability, navigation, or searchability.

To submit feedback regarding this documentation portal, please share your notes with the **College IT Cell** or your **System Administrator** during regular administrative hours.
