## Common Platform Issues & Solutions

### 1. Account Sign-In & Authentication

* **Issue:** "Access Denied" or redirected to a personal account.
  * **Solution:** Ensure you are signing in with your official college Google account ending in `@amrolicollege.ac.in`. Personal accounts (such as `@gmail.com`) cannot access JyotOS services.
* **Issue:** Multiple Google accounts causing sign-in confusion.
  * **Solution:** Open an Incognito or Private browsing window, navigate to JyotOS Central, and sign in exclusively with your college credentials.
* **Issue:** Outdated session data or blank loading screens.
  * **Solution:** Clear your web browser cache and cookies, or perform a hard refresh (`Ctrl + F5` on Windows).

### 2. Application Access in JyotOS Central

* **Issue:** An application tile displays a lock icon with a "Request Access" button.
  * **Solution:** Click **Request Access** on the application card. The request is queued for review by the System Administrator.
* **Issue:** An application tile is not visible at all.
  * **Solution:** Contact your System Administrator to verify whether the tool has been provisioned for your department or role.

### 3. StaffBridge Profile Verification

* **Issue:** Profile remains in "Submitted" status.
  * **Solution:** Department Coordinators review submissions for their respective departments. Contact your Department Coordinator to expedite verification.
* **Issue:** Status shows "Changes Requested".
  * **Solution:** Open your StaffBridge dashboard, review the feedback remarks provided by the reviewer, make the requested edits, and re-submit your profile.
