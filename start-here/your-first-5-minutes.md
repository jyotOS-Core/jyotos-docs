Welcome to the JyotOS ecosystem! Follow this quick 5-step checklist to get oriented and access your applications in under five minutes.

***

## 5-Minute Setup Checklist

```
[ Step 1: Sign In ] ──► [ Step 2: Check Profile ] ──► [ Step 3: Open Central ]
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
[ Step 5: Personalize ] ◄── [ Step 4: Launch App ] ◄───────────┘
```

### Step 1: Sign In with Your College Account

1. Open your web browser and go to [https://jyotos.vercel.app](https://jyotos.vercel.app).
2. Click **Sign in with College Account**.
3. Choose your official college Google account (`@amrolicollege.ac.in`).

### Step 2: Confirm Your Staff Profile is "Ready"

* If you are a returning staff member whose profile is already verified in StaffBridge, you will immediately reach your dashboard.
* If you are a new staff member, sign in to **StaffBridge** first to complete your onboarding form (personal details, department, qualification, photo, and signature).

### Step 3: Explore the JyotOS Central Launcher

* Review the application cards displayed on your screen.
* Applications with active launch buttons are ready to open immediately.
* Applications with a lock icon are available for discovery—you can request access if needed for your institutional duties.

### Step 4: Open Your Primary Everyday Application

* Click on your primary work tool—such as **StaffBridge** to review your profile and roster, or **ClassPulse Pro** to check your academic timetable.
* The application opens in a new tab with your identity already authenticated.

### Step 5: Personalize Your Portal

1. **Pin Favorites:** Click the star icon on applications you use every day to keep them at the top of your dashboard.
2. **Choose Theme:** Click your user avatar at the top right and select **Auto**, **Light**, or **Dark** mode according to your viewing preference.
