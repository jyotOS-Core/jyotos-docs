When you have a question or encounter an issue while using JyotOS applications, comprehensive self-service resources and institutional support channels are available to assist you.

***

## 1. Search the Help Center

You can quickly find answers across all documentation guides and FAQs using the search feature:

* Press **Ctrl + K** (on Windows) or **Cmd + K** (on macOS) at any time.
* Enter a keyword, application name, or error code (e.g., *"onboarding photo"*, *"locked tile"*, or *"SSO"*).
* Select any search result to jump directly to that section.

***

## 2. Self-Service Troubleshooting Guides

Before reporting an issue, consult the dedicated troubleshooting pages for step-by-step resolution:

* **JyotOS Central Issues:** See [Central Troubleshooting](../products/central/troubleshooting.md) for help with portal login alerts, locked cards, and access requests.
* **StaffBridge & Onboarding Issues:** Visit the [StaffBridge Troubleshooting Center](../products/staffbridge/troubleshooting/README.md) for assistance with document uploads, photo specifications, and profile verification status.
* **Frequently Asked Questions:** Review the [Frequently Asked Questions](../frequently-asked-questions.md) and [Central FAQ](../products/central/faq.md) for direct answers to common questions.

***

## 3. Institutional Support Contacts

If self-service guides do not resolve your problem, reach out to the appropriate institutional contact:

* **Department Verification & Rosters:** Contact your designated **Department Coordinator** if your profile verification is pending or if your department assignment requires correction.
* **Institutional Google Account:** Contact your college **IT Cell / System Administrator** if you cannot access your official `@amrolicollege.ac.in` Google account or password.
* **Platform Permissions & Roles:** Contact your **Platform Owner** or **System Administrator** if you need elevated access or role modifications in Central.

***

## 4. Submitting Suggestions & Feedback

We actively improve the JyotOS ecosystem based on user feedback from faculty, staff, and coordinators.

To share a suggestion, report an unexpected behavior, or propose an enhancement, visit the [Feedback & Suggestions](../feedback-and-suggestions.md) page.

***

## Best Practices When Reporting an Issue

To help administrators resolve your issue promptly, always provide:

1. **Application Name:** Which application were you using (e.g., JyotOS Central, StaffBridge)?
2. **Exact Message:** What was the exact text of the alert or error shown on screen?
3. **Action Taken:** What button or link did you click immediately before the issue occurred?
4. **Device & Browser:** Are you using a desktop or mobile device, and which browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)?
