# Department Coordinator & HOD Guide for JyotOS

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** Department Coordinators, Heads of Department (HODs), Committee Conveners
* **Applies To:** JyotOS Ecosystem (Departmental Administration)
* **Scope:** Department-scoped review queues and operational workflows
{% endhint %}

This guide summarizes responsibilities and workflows for department coordinators and academic leaders across the live applications of the JyotOS ecosystem.

---

## 1. JyotOS Central (App Launcher)

Coordinators use Central to access their department-scoped operational tools:

* **Access Granted Applications:** Launch specialized tools configured for your role.
* **Access Requests:** Request access to locked ecosystem tools when new departmental responsibilities arise.

→ See [Central Overview](../products/central/overview.md).

---

## 2. StaffBridge (Profile Verification)

Department Coordinators serve as the first-line reviewer for staff onboarding in their department:

* **Department Review Queue:** Inspect onboarding submissions for faculty and staff members mapped to your department.
* **Inspect Media:** Review uploaded staff photographs and digital signatures for clarity and correctness.
* **Request Corrections:** If submission details are incomplete, flag specific fields with written remarks for the staff member to revise.
* **Coordinator Verification:** Formally verify complete and accurate submissions to advance them to Administrator Verification.

→ See [StaffBridge Department Coordinator Guide](../products/staffbridge/coordinator-guide.md).

---

## 3. EventOS (Event Coordination)

Coordinators plan and propose academic and co-curricular events:

* **Propose Events:** Draft event details including title, multi-day schedule, campus venue, target audience, department eligibility, and capacity limits.
* **Submit for Approval:** Forward event proposals to the campus Principal for executive review.
* **Track Proposal Status:** Monitor proposal progress (Draft, Under Review, Changes Requested, Approved, Published).

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Coming soon:** Assigning same-campus session helpers, managing attendance scanning windows, and compiling post-event report packages are not available yet.
{% endhint %}

→ See [EventOS Coordinator Guide](../products/eventos/coordinator-guide.md).

---

## 4. ClassPulse Pro (Department Oversight)

Department Coordinators supervise departmental faculty rosters and curriculum delivery:

* **Department-Bound Scoping:** The workspace automatically scopes to your authorized department roster.
* **Department Aggregate KPIs:** Monitor total conducted lectures, weekly department load, and delivery rate against the 85% benchmark.
* **Faculty Roster Reviews:** Inspect individual instructor cards or table views to monitor lecture targets, conducted classes, and delivery variances.
* **Faculty Detail Inspection:** Review chronological diary records, timetable slot matches, and recorded syllabus topics.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Coming soon:** Single sign-on with JyotOS via StaffBridge launcher is not switched on yet for ClassPulse Pro. Coordinators sign in using their assigned pilot password.
{% endhint %}

→ See [HOD Department Center](../products/classpulse-pro/hod-department-center.md) and [Faculty Lecture Reconciler](../products/classpulse-pro/faculty-lecture-reconciler.md).
