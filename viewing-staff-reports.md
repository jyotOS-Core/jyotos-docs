## How Do I Search & Filter Staff Records?

Use the built-in filtering controls to narrow your view:

* **Campus Filter:** Filter records by **Amroli Campus** or **Chaprabhatha Campus**.
* **Department Filter:** Filter by specific academic or administrative departments.
* **Submission Status Filter:** Filter by **Submitted**, **Coordinator Verified**, **Needs Correction**, **Correction Submitted**, or **Admin Verified**.
* **Active Status Filter:** Filter by **Active** or **Inactive** records.
* **Instant Search Bar:** Search across staff records by Full Name, Official Email, Mobile Number, or Staff Reference ID.

***

## How Do I Export Staff Reports?

1. Apply your desired campus, department, or status filters on the Staff Reports page.
2. Review the filtered staff table.
3. Click **Export Staff Report**.
4. The system generates a downloadable CSV file containing complete verified staff roster details.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Data Governance Policy** Exported staff reports contain confidential employee information. Downloads must be handled securely in accordance with institutional data protection policies.
{% endhint %}
