Welcome to the **System Administrator Guide** for StaffBridge.

System Administrators and the College Principal hold executive governance over staff identity records across all campuses, departments, and connected institutional systems.

***

## Executive Responsibilities

As a System Administrator, your primary responsibilities include:

* **Final Institutional Verification:** Performing final administrative review and granting **Admin Verified** status.
* **Active Status Management:** Managing Active Status (**Active** / **Inactive**) for verified staff records.
* **Master Roster & Reporting:** Searching, filtering, and exporting master staff records across campuses via the **Staff Reports & Verification Roster** page.
* **System Communications:** Managing email campaigns and broadcast notifications under current system limits.

***

## Operational Scope Comparison

| Responsibility Area       |  Department Coordinator  |     System Administrator / Principal     |
| ------------------------- | :----------------------: | :--------------------------------------: |
| **Review Scope**          | Assigned Department Only |    College-Wide (All Campuses & Units)   |
| **Verification Level**    |   Coordinator Verified   |      Admin Verified (Final Approval)     |
| **Active Status Control** |           None           | Full Control (**Active** / **Inactive**) |
| **Master Roster Export**  |           None           |    Full CSV Export (**Staff Reports**)   |
