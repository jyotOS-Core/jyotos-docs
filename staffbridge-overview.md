StaffBridge is the staff identity, onboarding, verification and profile management platform of the JyotOS ecosystem.

It provides Amroli College with a secure, centralized system of record to manage staff details, streamline department verification, issue digital identification previews, and maintain trusted identity records across institutional campuses.

***

## Key Institutional Functions

StaffBridge supports six core institutional functions:

* **Collecting Onboarding Information:** Gathering complete personal, academic, and professional details through a unified online registration form.
* **Maintaining Staff Profiles:** Storing up-to-date staff credentials, qualifications, contact details, and identification media.
* **Verifying Submitted Information:** Enabling department coordinators and administrators to review and verify staff records.
* **Managing Corrections:** Providing structured workflows to request and submit field-level profile updates.
* **Maintaining Trusted Identity Records:** Ensuring accurate, single-source data for teaching and administrative personnel across campuses.
* **Providing Verified Staff Access:** Supplying verified staff identity information to supported JyotOS applications.

***

## Intended User Roles

StaffBridge supports four primary user groups across the institution:

1. **Staff Members:** Faculty and administrative personnel who submit onboarding registration and maintain profile accuracy.
2. **Department Coordinators:** Academic faculty designated to review and verify staff submissions within their assigned departments.
3. **Principal:** Executive institutional leadership overseeing college-wide staff rosters, verification progress, and access rights.
4. **System Administrators:** IT administrators responsible for user access management, institutional settings, email communications, and system integration.

***

## How StaffBridge Fits into JyotOS

StaffBridge operates as the trusted identity foundation for the institutional ecosystem:

```
StaffBridge  ──►  Verified Staff Identity  ──►  Eligible JyotOS Applications
```

Staff records verified within StaffBridge serve as the single source of truth. Once a staff record is fully approved by administration, their verified identity information enables access to supported institutional applications within the JyotOS ecosystem (such as EventOS).

{% hint style="info" %}
**Integration Eligibility** Only staff records with **Admin Verified** status and active profiles are eligible for access to connected JyotOS applications.
{% endhint %}
