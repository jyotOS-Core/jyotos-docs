# Getting Started with ClassPulse Pro

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** All academic personnel, faculty members, coordinators, and administrators
* **Applies To:** ClassPulse Pro (First-Time Orientation)
* **Access Requirement:** Verified college email and pilot access assignment
{% endhint %}

This guide provides an introductory orientation to ClassPulse Pro, covering sign-in procedures, interface navigation, date range controls, and role-based workspaces.

---

## Before You Begin

Before accessing ClassPulse Pro, ensure you have:

* An active college email address ending in `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.
* Your assigned pilot login password provided by your Department Coordinator or System Administrator.
* A modern web browser on your computer, tablet, or smartphone.

---

## Signing In for the Pilot

During the initial pilot period, sign in directly using your assigned credentials:

1. Open the ClassPulse Pro sign-in page.
2. In the **Official College Email ID** field, enter your institutional email (`name@amrolicollege.ac.in`).
3. In the **Password** field, enter your password.
4. Click **Sign In**.
5. Upon verification, the portal opens to your role-specific workspace or the universal Module Launchpad.

## Interface Layout Overview

ClassPulse Pro features a clean, responsive layout designed for both desktop workstations and mobile devices:

### 1. Top Navigation Bar
* **Location Breadcrumb:** Displays your current location within the portal (e.g., `ClassPulse / My Teaching Portal`).
* **Page Title:** Shows the active workspace or view name.
* **Sync Indicator:** Displays a live synchronization badge. When data recalculates dynamically, a temporary syncing indicator confirms that live records are being refreshed.
* **Action Buttons:** Contextual buttons such as **Export CSV** or **Print Diary** when viewing detailed reports.
* **User Profile & Logout:** Displays your name and role badge, with a **Logout** button to end your active session.

### 2. Navigation Sidebar
The collapsible left sidebar groups features into core operational categories:

* **Teaching & Workload:** Direct access to **My Teaching Portal** for faculty members.
* **Academic Overview:** Access to **Principal Executive View**, **Subject Division Matrix**, **HOD Department Center**, and **Faculty Reconciler**.
* **Calendar & Review:** Access to the **Academic Calendar & Holidays** schedule.
* **Operations & Admin:** Access to administrative tools for master file uploads and user permissions.

### 3. Module Launchpad
On the home screen, the **Module Launchpad** presents available workspaces as interactive cards with search and category filters, allowing quick access to all relevant academic tools.

## Using the Date Tracker Bar

Academic delivery metrics in ClassPulse Pro are calculated dynamically relative to a selected time window:

1. Locate the **Date Tracker Bar** near the top of the workspace.
2. Click one of the preset date buttons:
   * **June (15–30):** Early term teaching window.
   * **July (Full):** Complete July instructional period.
   * **August (Full):** Complete August instructional period.
   * **Term to Date:** Cumulative progress from term commencement to the present date.
3. Review the **Teaching Days** indicator, which automatically calculates effective working days by subtracting Sundays and official institutional holidays stored in the college calendar.
4. When switching date periods, all metrics, delivery percentages, and faculty targets refresh automatically.

---

## Role-Based Workspaces

What you see in ClassPulse Pro depends on your assigned institutional role:

* **Teaching Faculty:** Opens directly to **My Teaching Portal**, displaying your personal timetable hours, logged sessions, and teacher's diary.
* **Department Coordinators:** Opens with departmental access, allowing you to monitor faculty rosters and delivery benchmarks for your assigned department.
* **Principal & Administrators:** Provides full college-wide access, including cross-department comparisons, division parity analysis, and executive delivery charts.
