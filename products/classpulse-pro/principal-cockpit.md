# Principal Executive View in ClassPulse Pro

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** College Principal, Vice-Principal, Campus Directors, Executive Leadership
* **Applies To:** ClassPulse Pro (Executive Leadership Overview)
* **Scope:** College-wide delivery benchmarks across all departments and campuses
{% endhint %}

The **Principal Executive View** provides top institutional leadership with a macro-level overview of curriculum delivery, teaching hours, and departmental performance across Amroli Campus and Chaprabhatha Campus.

---

## Executive Objectives

Institutional leaders use this dashboard to answer high-level academic governance questions:

* Is the college as a whole meeting its 85% timetable delivery target?
* Which academic departments are on schedule, and which are accumulating teaching deficits?
* Are there unexplained dips in daily teaching volume across campuses?
* Which instructors or departments require scheduling adjustments or makeup sessions?

---

## Dual-Mode View Switcher

On the main landing page, executive accounts have access to a view switcher in the header:

* **Module Launchpad:** Displays the full catalog of eleven workspace cards with quick search, role badges, and category filters.
* **Principal Executive View:** Switches directly into the executive analytics view with college-wide delivery rates, velocity curves, and departmental comparison charts.

---

## Key Dashboard Sections

The executive view organizes institutional indicators into structured sections:

### 1. College-Wide Timetable Delivery Rate
The primary hero card displays the aggregate institution-wide delivery percentage:

* **Institutional Target:** The college delivery benchmark is established at **85.0%**.
* **Status Indicator:**
  * **On Track (Green):** Overall conducted lectures meet or exceed the 85% benchmark.
  * **Deficit (Red):** Overall conducted lectures fall below 85%, indicating an institutional delivery gap.
* **Volume Metrics:** Displays total conducted lecture hours alongside total expected contract hours for the selected date window (e.g., *9,742 conducted of 11,350 expected hours*).

### 2. Faculty Distribution & Net Variance
Complementary metric cards summarize academic workforce capacity:

* **Active Faculty Count:** Total instructors actively teaching across all shifts (e.g., 87 instructors, comprising single-contract and dual-contract faculty).
* **Workload Variance:** Shows the net institutional lecture gap across all four academic departments. A negative variance indicates missed periods due to events, leaves, or cancellations.
* **Working Days Count:** Confirms the active count of teaching days in the selected date window after excluding Sundays and recognized college holidays.

### 3. Department Delivery Comparison Chart
A visual comparison chart displays side-by-side performance across all four academic departments:

* BBA & M.Com EM
* B.Com EM & GM
* BCA Noon (Afternoon Shift)
* BCA Morning / B.Sc Data Science / M.Sc IT

Leadership can immediately identify which departments are operating above the 85% benchmark and which require departmental coordination.

### 4. Daily Teaching Velocity Chart
The velocity chart plots the daily volume of conducted lectures across the selected calendar period:

* **Normal Daily Patterns:** Visualizes expected Monday through Saturday teaching volumes.
* **Holiday Drops:** Reflects planned non-instructional days where lecture activity drops to zero.
* **Anomaly Detection:** Highlights unexpected mid-week dips that may correspond to weather disruptions, campus events, or widespread staff absence.

---

## Executive Decision Actions

Using the telemetry provided in the executive view, leadership can take targeted action:

1. **Review Departmental Pacing:** Compare delivery rates between shifts (e.g., BCA Morning vs BCA Afternoon) to ensure balanced student preparation.
2. **Review Dual-Contract Allocations:** Monitor instructors teaching across multiple degree programs to verify that combined obligations remain achievable.
3. **Initiate Departmental Remediation:** When a department falls below the 85% benchmark, the Principal can direct the Department Coordinator to schedule makeup lectures before examination preparation periods.
