## 1. General and Access

### What is ClassPulse Pro?
ClassPulse Pro is the academic workload reconciliation, timetable delivery tracking, and cross-division curriculum delivery balance platform for Amroli College across its Amroli and Chaprabhatha campuses.

### Who can access ClassPulse Pro?
ClassPulse Pro is currently available to designated pilot teaching faculty, Department Coordinators, and institutional leadership. Access is being introduced progressively in stages.

### How do I sign in during the pilot phase?
Sign in on the ClassPulse Pro login page using your official college email (`name@amrolicollege.ac.in`) and your assigned pilot password.

## 2. Delivery Targets and Benchmarks

### What is the institutional delivery benchmark?
Amroli College establishes an institutional delivery benchmark of **85.0%**. Any academic department or instructor conducting fewer than 85% of expected teaching hours within a given period is identified as operating at a delivery deficit.

### How is the delivery rate calculated?
The delivery rate is calculated by dividing actual conducted sessions by expected teaching hours for the selected date window, expressed as a percentage:
$$\text{Delivery Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Actual Conducted Sessions}}{\text{Expected Hours}} \right) \times 100$$

### What happens if an instructor or department falls below 85%?
When an instructor or department drops below the 85% benchmark, the portal highlights the pacing gap with an amber or red status indicator. Department Coordinators review these indicators and schedule compensatory lectures before examination preparation periods.

## 3. Academic Calendar and Working Days

### How are institutional working days calculated?
The system calculates effective working days for any selected date window by starting with all calendar days and subtracting:
* All Sundays.
* All state, gazetted, and national holidays.
* Official college-declared non-instructional days stored in the academic calendar.

### Does a declared holiday reduce expected teaching hours?
Yes. When a holiday occurs, the working day count decreases, which automatically reduces expected teaching hours for that week. Instructors are not penalized for college-sanctioned holidays.

## 4. Timetable and Teaching Diaries

### How often are teaching records synchronized?
Teaching diary records and timetable allocations are synchronized continuously with the central database. The sync indicator in the top navigation bar confirms that live records are current.

### Can a Department Coordinator alter an instructor's diary entries?
No. Daily diary entries represent direct teacher records. Coordinators can review entries, inspect timetable slot matches, and request adjustments through academic channels, but cannot directly edit submitted session entries.

### What should I do if my teaching diary shows an unlinked timetable notice?
If your profile displays an unlinked timetable notice, contact your Department Coordinator with your name and course papers. The coordinator will link your instructor profile to your assigned timetable slots.

## 5. Cross-Division Pacing and Division Alignment

### What causes pacing gaps between parallel divisions?
Pacing gaps commonly arise when:
* Campus holidays fall disproportionately on days of the week when a specific division has scheduled lectures.
* Unexpected instructor leave occurs without immediate substitute arrangement.
* Practical laboratory sessions require rescheduling due to equipment maintenance.

### What is an Equalization Directive?
An Equalization Directive is an administrative notice generated through the **Subject Division Matrix** when the lecture gap between parallel divisions exceeds three sessions. It identifies the lagging cohort and specifies the required compensatory classes needed to restore balanced progress.

## 6. Data Exports and Reports

### Can reconciliation data be exported for academic meetings?
Yes. The **Faculty Reconciler** and **Subject Division Matrix** support compact views that can be printed or saved directly to PDF using your browser's print function (`Ctrl + P` or `Cmd + P`).

### Can department rosters be exported as a spreadsheet?
Yes. Coordinators and administrators can click the **Export CSV** button in the top navigation bar to download the current roster and delivery metrics for external analysis.
