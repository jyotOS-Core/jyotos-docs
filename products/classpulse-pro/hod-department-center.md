# HOD Department Center in ClassPulse Pro

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** Department Coordinators, Heads of Department (HODs)
* **Applies To:** ClassPulse Pro (Departmental Leadership Workspace)
* **Scope:** Faculty rosters and curriculum delivery within your assigned department
{% endhint %}

The **HOD Department Center** is the departmental leadership workspace where coordinators supervise faculty rosters, monitor weekly teaching loads, evaluate curriculum delivery rates, and review lecture progress across courses.

---

## Department Leadership Scoping

To maintain clear organizational boundaries, ClassPulse Pro applies role-based scoping:

* **Department Coordinator View:** When a coordinator signs in, the workspace automatically focuses on their assigned department (such as BBA & M.Com EM, B.Com, BCA Afternoon, or BCA Morning & IT). Access to other department rosters is restricted.
* **Executive Leadership View:** The College Principal and System Administrators can freely switch between all department views using the department tabs at the top of the workspace.

---

## Department Aggregate KPIs

The summary strip at the top of the workspace highlights key departmental indicators for the selected date window:

* **Department Faculty:** Total number of instructors actively assigned teaching hours in the department.
* **Conducted Sessions:** Aggregate count of classroom lectures, laboratory sessions, and tutorials completed within the selected period.
* **Weekly Department Load:** Total weekly teaching periods allocated across all departmental timetable schedules.
* **Department Delivery Rate:** The overall percentage of scheduled lectures conducted:
  $$\text{Department Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Conducted Lectures}}{\text{Total Expected Hours}} \right) \times 100$$
  * Rates of **85% or higher** meet institutional delivery standards.
  * Rates below **85%** indicate an overall department delivery gap requiring review.

---

## Inspecting the Faculty Roster

Below the metric strip, coordinators can inspect instructors in either **Card View** or **Table View**:

### A. Card View (Default View)
The workspace initializes in Card View, presenting a dedicated card for each instructor:

* **Instructor Name & Badges:** Displays the instructor's name alongside special contract tags (such as `Dual Contract` for faculty teaching across multiple departments, or `Visiting` for external lecturers).
* **Timetable Link Badge:** Confirms whether the instructor's diary entries are connected to the official master timetable.
* **Four-Metric Summary:**
  * **Load:** Weekly contracted teaching periods (e.g., `18/w`).
  * **Target:** Expected lectures in the selected date window.
  * **Taken:** Actual conducted sessions logged in teaching diaries.
  * **Variance:** Difference between conducted sessions and target (e.g., `+2` surplus or `-6` gap).
* **Delivery Rate & Teaching Days:** Displays the individual delivery percentage alongside active teaching days out of total working days (e.g., `92% (19/21d)`).

### B. Table View
Click the table icon in the view controls to switch to a spreadsheet view. This mode allows sorting instructors by weekly load, conducted sessions, or delivery percentage.

---

## Reviewing Faculty Details and Diaries

Clicking on any instructor card or table row opens the **Faculty Detail Modal**:

1. **Daily Teaching Register:** Shows a chronological list of all sessions conducted by the instructor, including date, time period, division, and subject.
2. **Timetable Matching:** Verifies whether each session matches scheduled timetable allocations or represents an extra or substitute class.
3. **Syllabus Coverage:** Displays the exact curriculum topics, chapters, and units recorded in the instructor's diary for each session.

---

## Managing Teaching Variances

When inspecting faculty cards, coordinators monitor delivery variances:

* **Positive Variance (`+`):** Indicates the instructor conducted additional tutorial or substitute hours.
* **Minor Gap (0 to -4):** Common due to individual leave or minor scheduling adjustments.
* **Larger Gap (-5 or lower):** Flags a pacing deficit. Coordinators should review the instructor's schedule and plan makeup sessions or practical laboratory periods to restore curriculum progress before term examinations.
