# StaffBridge Overview

StaffBridge is the centralized staff identity, onboarding, verification, and profile management platform for the JyotOS ecosystem.

It provides educational institutions like Amroli College with an authoritative, single source of truth for managing institutional staff records, streamlining department verification, issuing digital identification previews, and managing field-level profile corrections.

***

## Identity Authority for JyotOS

StaffBridge operates as the identity authority across all connected JyotOS applications:

```
StaffBridge (Onboarding & Verification)
   │
   ▼
Verified Staff Identity ("Ready" / Admin Verified)
   │
   ▼
JyotOS Central SSO & Connected Campus Applications (EventOS, ClassPulse Pro, etc.)
```

1. **Official Institutional Sign-In:** Staff sign in once using their assigned college Google Workspace account (`@amrolicollege.ac.in`).
2. **Identity Verification:** Staff profiles undergo two-tier review: department verification by a Department Coordinator, followed by final institutional approval by an Administrator or Principal.
3. **The "Ready" Requirement:** Only staff members whose profiles have attained **Admin Verified** status ("Ready") are eligible for automated single sign-on access across connected JyotOS applications.

***

## Key Institutional Functions

StaffBridge supports six core institutional functions:

* **Staff Onboarding:** Gathers comprehensive personal, academic, professional, and emergency contact details through a structured onboarding form.
* **Profile Management:** Maintains staff credentials, appointment categories, teaching/administrative designations, and identification media.
* **Two-Tier Verification:** Enables Department Coordinators and System Administrators to review and verify submitted data against official appointment letters.
* **Structured Corrections:** Provides a field-level correction workflow where reviewers can flag specific data points or media files with actionable comments.
* **Digital ID Card Previews:** Automatically generates real-time institutional staff ID card previews featuring passport photos, signatures, blood groups, and QR codes.
* **Campus & Department Mapping:** Maps personnel across multiple institutional campuses (Amroli Campus and Chaprabhatha Campus) and primary or secondary academic departments.

***

## Intended User Roles

StaffBridge supports four distinct roles:

1. **Staff Members:** Faculty and non-teaching personnel who complete initial registration, track verification status, preview their institutional ID cards, and respond to correction requests.
2. **Department Coordinators:** Academic faculty designated to review submissions from their assigned departments, verify qualifications and department mappings, or request corrections.
3. **Principal:** Institutional executive leadership with full visibility over institutional staff rosters, cross-campus analytics, and verification approvals.
4. **System Administrators:** IT Cell administrators responsible for final profile verification, platform access control, user accounts, broadcast communications, and data exports.
