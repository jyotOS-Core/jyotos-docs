# Reviewing Staff Profiles

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** Department Coordinators
* **Applies To:** StaffBridge
* **Review Target:** Profiles with status Submitted or Correction Submitted
{% endhint %}

This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough for Department Coordinators to open their department review queue, inspect pending staff records, and evaluate submitted details.

---

## Accessing the Department Review Queue

1. Open your browser and navigate to the StaffBridge login page.
2. Sign in using your official Google `@amrolicollege.ac.in` account.
3. Access the **Admin Portal** from the navigation menu. Your view automatically filters to show staff records within your assigned department.

---

## Step-by-Step Review Process

### Step 1: Filter Pending Submissions

In the review queue, apply the status filter **Submitted** or **Correction Submitted** to view profiles waiting for department review.

### Step 2: Open a Staff Record

Click on a staff member's row or reference ID (`STAFF-2026-XXXX`) to open their complete profile review sheet.

### Step 3: Evaluate Personal and Contact Information

Verify the following details:
* **Full Name:** Formatted correctly (e.g. `Dr. A. K. Sharma` or `Prof. P. V. Desai`).
* **Date of Birth:** Matches official appointment records.
* **Email Address:** Ends strictly with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.
* **Mobile Number:** Valid 10-digit primary contact number.
* **Residential Address:** Complete address including 6-digit PIN code.

### Step 4: Verify Employment and Department Assignment

Confirm the employment fields match institutional appointment records:
* **Primary Campus:** Amroli Campus or Chaprabhatha Campus.
* **Primary Department:** Assigned department within your coordinator scope.
* **Staff Category:** Teaching Staff or Non-Teaching Staff.
* **Official Designation:** Current appointed designation.
* **Date of Joining:** Official commencement date.

### Step 5: Check Academic Qualifications

Cross-check selected qualifications (e.g., M.A., M.Com., M.Sc., Ph.D., NET, GSET) against college appointment files.

### Step 6: Inspect Identification Media

Inspect the uploaded media items:
* **Passport Photo:** Clear, front-facing, centered, with adequate lighting and a plain, light background.
* **Signature Image:** Clear, continuous, dark ink on a plain white surface or drawn legibly on the digital signature pad.

### Step 7: Decide on the Record

* If all details and media are complete and accurate: Proceed to [Verifying a Staff Profile](verifying-a-staff-profile.md).
* If any field contains errors or requires updates: Proceed to [Requesting Corrections](requesting-corrections.md).
