# Troubleshoot Photo and Signature

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** Staff Members Uploading or Capturing Media
* **Applies To:** StaffBridge Media Capture & Canvas
* **Limits:** Photo max 2 MB (JPEG/PNG/WebP), Signature max 1 MB (PNG/JPEG)
{% endhint %}

This guide helps resolve technical difficulties encountered while capturing passport photos or providing digital signatures in StaffBridge.

---

## Common Media Issues and Solutions

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Camera View Black / Not Opening** | Browser camera permission blocked | Open browser site settings, set **Camera** to **Allow**, and reload the page. |
| **Photo Upload Fails** | File size exceeds 2 MB or unsupported format | Resize image below 2 MB. Acceptable formats are JPEG, PNG, and WebP. |
| **Signature Canvas Unresponsive** | Touch or stylus not registered | Tap **Clear Canvas** and draw your signature smoothly inside the white box boundaries. |
| **Uploaded Signature Rejected** | Blurry image, low contrast, or lined paper | Sign with dark blue or black ink on unlined white paper in bright lighting, or draw directly on the canvas. |
| **Crop Tool Fails to Load** | Browser WebGL or hardware acceleration disabled | Check browser settings or upload a pre-cropped square passport photo directly. |

## Media Best Practices

* **Passport Photo:**
  * Clean, plain light background (white, off-white, or light blue).
  * Direct forward-facing view with both ears and shoulders visible.
  * Neutral facial expression, good lighting, no heavy filters or selfie angles.
* **Signature Image:**
  * Dark ink on clean white background.
  * Crop tightly around signature strokes.
  * Keep file size under 1 MB.
