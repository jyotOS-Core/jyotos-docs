# Troubleshoot Staff Onboarding

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** Staff Members Completing Onboarding
* **Applies To:** StaffBridge Onboarding Form
* **Key Requirements:** Official email (`@amrolicollege.ac.in`), 10-digit mobile, valid address PIN code
{% endhint %}

This guide provides troubleshooting solutions for issues encountered while filling out or submitting the staff onboarding registration form.

---

## Common Form Issues and Solutions

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Form Won't Submit** | Missing mandatory field or format validation failure | Check for fields highlighted with a red border. Ensure mobile number has 10 digits and address includes a 6-digit PIN code. |
| **Email Field Rejected** | Personal email address entered | Enter your official college email ending with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`. Personal emails are not accepted. |
| **Department List Empty** | Primary Campus not selected | Select your Primary Campus (**Amroli Campus** or **Chaprabhatha Campus**) first. The department dropdown populates based on the chosen campus. |
| **"Duplicate Registration" Alert** | Profile already exists under this email or mobile number | An onboarding record has already been submitted. Sign in to your dashboard to view your profile status. |
| **Photo or Signature Missing** | Required media files not captured or drawn | Complete both media requirements: capture or upload a passport photo and provide your digital signature. |

---

## Error Messages Explained

* **"Please enter a valid 10-digit mobile number":**\
  The mobile number field requires exactly 10 numeric digits. Do not include country codes (`+91`), dashes, or spaces.
* **"Duplicate registration detected":**\
  A profile already exists for the provided email address or mobile number. If you need to make changes, sign in to your dashboard to review correction requests rather than submitting a new form.
* **"Primary Campus must be selected":**\
  You must choose between Amroli Campus and Chaprabhatha Campus before selecting your academic or administrative department.
