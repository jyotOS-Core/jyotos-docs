# Using Your Staff Dashboard

This guide explains how to navigate your StaffBridge personal dashboard, view your digital ID card preview, track profile review progress, and access the institutional staff directory.

***

## Accessing Your Dashboard

1. Open StaffBridge in your web browser.
2. Click **Sign in with Google** and choose your official college account ending in `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.
3. Upon authentication, you are directed to your personal staff dashboard.

***

## Key Dashboard Features

### 1. Profile Overview Card

The top card summarizes your current employment identity:

* **Staff Reference ID:** Your assigned institutional identifier (e.g. `STAFF-2026-0086`).
* **Profile Status Badge:** Color-coded status indicator showing your review stage (Submitted, Coordinator Verified, Needs Correction, Correction Submitted, or Admin Verified).
* **Primary Campus & Department:** Your appointed campus location (Amroli Campus or Chaprabhatha Campus) and primary department.
* **Designation & Category:** Your official appointment title and employment classification.

### 2. Live Digital ID Card Preview

Your dashboard renders a real-time preview of your institutional staff ID card:

* Front side displaying your passport portrait photograph, full name, designation, primary department, campus, and blood group.
* Reverse side displaying emergency contact details, date of joining, digital signature, and verification QR code.
* The preview reflects the media and information you submitted during onboarding.

### 3. Pending Corrections Panel

If a Department Coordinator or Administrator requests a correction on any of your profile fields or media, a dedicated **Pending Correction Fields** section appears directly on your dashboard. You can review reviewer comments, enter updated information, and submit corrections from this panel.

### 4. Push Notification Controls

StaffBridge supports browser-based Web Push alerts:

* Toggle push notifications on or off using the push subscription control on your dashboard.
* Enabling notifications delivers instant alerts to your phone or desktop when a coordinator or administrator verifies your profile or submits a correction request.

### 5. Staff Directory Navigation

The navigation bar on your dashboard includes a link to the **Staff Directory**. The directory allows you to browse colleagues across academic departments and campuses with official contact information.

***

## Dashboard Feature Summary

| Feature | Primary Purpose | Staff Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Status Badge** | Tracks verification progress | Monitor until status reaches Admin Verified ("Ready") |
| **ID Card Preview** | Visual preview of your staff ID card | Verify photo, signature, blood group, and emergency contact |
| **Correction Panel** | Displays flagged items | Enter corrected details and click Submit Update |
| **Push Controls** | Manages browser alerts | Toggle browser alerts for real-time review updates |
| **Staff Directory** | Browse institutional colleagues | Click Staff Directory to look up departmental faculty |
