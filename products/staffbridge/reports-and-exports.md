# Reports and Exports

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** System Administrators, College Principal, Leadership
* **Applies To:** StaffBridge
* **Export Formats:** CSV, XLS, PDF / Print roster
{% endhint %}

The **Reports & Exports** section provides tools for searching, filtering, and exporting college-wide staff records across both Amroli Campus and Chaprabhatha Campus.

---

## Accessing Reports & Exports

1. Sign in with an administrator account.
2. Select **Reports & Exports** from the administrator navigation menu.

---

## Searching and Filtering Records

Use the filter controls at the top of the roster to narrow down staff records:

* **Campus Filter:** View records for **Amroli Campus**, **Chaprabhatha Campus**, or all campuses combined.
* **Department Filter:** Filter by specific academic or administrative departments.
* **Staff Category:** Select Teaching Staff or Non-Teaching Staff.
* **Profile Status:** Filter by lifecycle state (Submitted, Coordinator Verified, Needs Correction, Correction Submitted, or Admin Verified).
* **Active Status:** Filter by `Active` or `Inactive` personnel.
* **Search Field:** Search instantly by staff name, reference ID (`STAFF-2026-XXXX`), official email address, or mobile number.

---

## Exporting Staff Records

StaffBridge supports three export methods:

1. **Export to CSV:** Generates a standard comma-separated values file suitable for spreadsheet applications and external data processing.
2. **Export to XLS:** Generates an Excel-compatible spreadsheet file formatted with column headers.
3. **Print / PDF Summary:** Formats the currently filtered roster for clean printing or saving as a PDF document.

### How to Generate an Export

1. Apply your desired filters (campus, department, status).
2. Confirm the record count in the results table.
3. Click the export button corresponding to your preferred format (**Export CSV**, **Export XLS**, or **Print / PDF**).
4. The file downloads directly to your local device.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Handling Exported Files**
Exported files contain staff details. Keep them private and share them only with people who need them.
{% endhint %}
