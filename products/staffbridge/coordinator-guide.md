# Department Coordinator Guide

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** Department Coordinators, Academic Department Heads
* **Applies To:** StaffBridge
* **Scope:** Assigned department(s) only
* **Primary Actions:** Review, request corrections, grant department verification
{% endhint %}

Welcome to the **Department Coordinator Guide** for StaffBridge.

Department Coordinators are designated academic faculty members responsible for reviewing, verifying, and managing staff onboarding submissions within their assigned academic or administrative departments.

---

## Role and Responsibilities

As a Department Coordinator, your primary responsibilities include:

* **Reviewing Submissions:** Evaluating incoming registrations for faculty and staff assigned to your department.
* **Verifying Details:** Confirming staff category, designation, date of joining, and academic qualifications against college appointment records.
* **Requesting Corrections:** Flagging inaccurate or incomplete fields and providing clear, actionable remarks to staff members.
* **Granting Department Verification:** Approving verified records to advance their status to **Coordinator Verified**.

---

## Access Scope and Boundaries

* **What You Can Access:** Submissions, profile details, uploaded media, and correction responses for personnel mapped to your assigned department.
* **What You Cannot Access:** Submissions belonging to other academic departments, college-wide settings, user role administration, or system-level configuration.
