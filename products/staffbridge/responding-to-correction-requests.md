# Responding to Correction Requests

This guide explains how to view, update, and resubmit information if an administrator or department coordinator requests a correction on your StaffBridge profile.

***

## How You Are Notified

If a reviewer flags an item on your profile, the status changes to **Needs Correction**. You are notified through two channels:

1. **Email Notification:** An automated notification is sent to your official `@amrolicollege.ac.in` email address listing the flagged fields and reviewer comments.
2. **Dashboard Alert:** A prominent **Pending Correction Fields** card appears when you sign in to your dashboard.

***

## Step-by-Step Response Procedure

Follow these steps to update your flagged information:

### Step 1: Sign In to Your Dashboard

1. Navigate to the StaffBridge login page.
2. Click **Sign in with Google** and select your official `@amrolicollege.ac.in` institutional account.
3. You will be redirected to your personal dashboard.

### Step 2: Locate the Pending Correction Section

On your dashboard, look for the **Pending Correction Fields** section. Each flagged field displays:

* **Field Name:** The specific data point that needs updating (e.g. `Mobile Number`, `Residential Address`, `Passport Photo`, `Qualifications`).
* **Reviewer's Comment:** Specific feedback from the coordinator or administrator explaining what needs to be changed (e.g. _"Please provide full 6-digit residential pincode"_ or _"Passport photo has dark background; please re-upload with light background"_).

### Step 3: Enter the Corrected Information

Input your updated details in the provided field:

* **Text Fields:** Enter the corrected text (e.g. updated address or telephone number).
* **Dropdowns & Selectors:** Choose the correct option from the menu.
* **Qualifications:** Update your qualification checkboxes.
* **Photo or Signature Re-Upload:** If a media item was flagged, upload a new photo file, upload a clean signature image, or redraw your signature using the digital canvas.

### Step 4: Add Optional Remarks

You can enter an optional response remark explaining the correction you made (e.g. _"Updated with current address and 6-digit pincode"_).

### Step 5: Submit Your Update

Click **Submit Update**. The system updates your profile status to **Correction Submitted** and queues your update for reviewer approval.

***

## What Happens After You Submit

1. An administrator or coordinator reviews your updated values alongside the original submission.
2. Once approved, the new information automatically replaces the old values in your official staff master record.
3. When all pending items are resolved and approved, your profile advances to **Admin Verified**, establishing your "Ready" status across the JyotOS ecosystem.
