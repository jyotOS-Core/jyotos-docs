# Requesting Corrections

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** Department Coordinators, System Administrators
* **Applies To:** StaffBridge
* **Resulting Status:** Needs Correction (`needs_correction`)
{% endhint %}

This guide explains when and how to flag field-level errors on a staff profile and how to write clear, actionable reviewer remarks.

---

## When to Request a Correction

Request a field correction whenever submitted information is inaccurate, incomplete, or fails visual standards:

* **Incomplete Addresses:** Missing 6-digit PIN code or landmark details.
* **Formatting Errors:** Name entered in lowercase or missing official prefix.
* **Media Quality Issues:** Blurry portrait photo, dark background, or cropped signature.
* **Category Mismatch:** Incorrect staff category or designation selected.
* **Employment Discrepancies:** Incorrect date of joining or campus assignment.

---

## Writing Effective Reviewer Remarks

Reviewer remarks are displayed directly to the staff member on their dashboard. Remarks must be **polite**, **specific**, and **instructional**.

### Reviewer Remark Guidance

| Field Issue | Ineffective Remark (Vague) | Effective Remark (Actionable) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Address PIN Code** | *"Wrong address."* | *"Please enter your complete residential address including your 6-digit PIN code."* |
| **Signature Media** | *"Fix signature."* | *"Please upload a clear signature image drawn with dark ink on plain white paper."* |
| **Qualification** | *"Check degrees."* | *"Please select M.Sc. and specify your specialization subject under Other Qualifications."* |
| **Designation** | *"Incorrect role."* | *"Please select Assistant Professor as your official appointment designation."* |

---

## How to Request a Correction

[SCREENSHOT TO CAPTURE: Field correction selection panel with reviewer remark entry]

1. Open the staff profile sheet in the Admin Portal.
2. Check the box next to each specific field requiring correction.
3. Enter actionable reviewer remarks in the comment box.
4. Click **Request Correction**.
5. The staff profile status automatically transitions to **Needs Correction** (`needs_correction`).
6. The staff member can now edit only the flagged fields and resubmit their profile.

---

## Reviewing Resubmissions

* When the staff member resubmits updated information, their status changes to **Correction Submitted** (`correction_submitted`).
* Open the profile in the review queue to evaluate the updated entries alongside your original remarks.
* If satisfied, approve the profile to **Coordinator Verified** (`coordinator_verified`).
* If issues remain, you can request further corrections following the same steps.
