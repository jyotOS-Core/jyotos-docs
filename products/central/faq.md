This section provides direct answers to frequently asked questions about JyotOS Central.

***

## General Questions

### What is JyotOS Central?
JyotOS Central is the unified application launchpad and portal for the JyotOS ecosystem, accessible at [https://jyotos.vercel.app](https://jyotos.vercel.app). It allows institutional faculty and staff to discover, launch, and request access to campus applications from one place.

### Do I need a separate username or password for Central?
No. Central uses your official college Google workspace account (`@amrolicollege.ac.in`) via StaffBridge Single Sign-On (SSO). You do not need to create or remember a separate password.

### Who is eligible to sign in to Central?
Any active faculty or staff member with an official institutional Google account whose StaffBridge profile has completed onboarding and received administrator verification ("Ready" status).

***

## Access & Permissions

### Why do some applications show a lock icon?
Applications displaying a lock icon are part of the institutional catalog but require explicit permission to launch. This allows you to discover tools available across the college while preserving access governance for specialized systems.

### How do I request access to a locked application?
Click the locked application card, enter a brief justification in the request window, and click **Submit Request**. An institutional administrator will review your submission and grant access if approved.

### Does gaining access in Central make me an administrator in that application?
No. Central controls *portal launch access* only. Roles and permissions inside each application (such as HOD, Coordinator, Evaluator, or Administrator) are managed independently within that application.

### Can I launch applications directly without going through Central?
Yes. If you have bookmarked an individual application URL (such as StaffBridge or ClassPulse Pro), you can navigate to it directly. You will be authenticated via the same Single Sign-On flow.

***

## Interface & Personalization

### How do I switch to dark mode?
Click your profile avatar or the menu icon in the top-right navigation bar, locate the **Theme** section, and select **Dark**. The change takes effect immediately without reloading.

### What does the "Auto" theme setting do?
The **Auto** setting automatically matches your operating system appearance. If your computer or phone switches to dark mode in the evening, Central adapts automatically.

### How do I pin my most frequently used applications?
Click the **Star icon** on any application card. Starred applications automatically appear in the **Favorites** section at the top of your dashboard.

### Can I use Central on my mobile phone?
Yes. Central is fully responsive and automatically switches to an optimized two-column mobile view with touch-friendly tiles on smartphone screens.

***

## Account & Identity

### Why does Central show my name and department?
When you sign in, Central reads your verified name and department affiliation from StaffBridge to personalize your dashboard and help administrators route requests. This information is read-only in Central.

### How do I correct my name or department if it displays incorrectly?
Update your profile information in StaffBridge and submit it for verification. Once approved by your coordinator, your updated name and department will display in Central upon your next sign-in.
