# Principal Guide for EventOS

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** College Principal, Campus Directors, Executive Leadership
* **Applies To:** EventOS (Principal Approvals Workspace)
* **Scope:** Campus-wide approval authority for event proposals, revisions, and reports
{% endhint %}

This guide explains how the College Principal and campus leadership review and decide event proposals, manage event revisions, approve post-event report packages, and exercise event cancellation authority.

---

## Principal Workspace Overview

The Principal workspace centralizes executive review queues for your campus:

* **Event Proposals Queue:** New and revised event proposals awaiting institutional approval.
* **Report Packages Queue:** Consolidated post-event reports submitted by coordinators awaiting final sign-off.
* **Published Events Overview:** Live catalog of currently approved and ongoing campus events.

---

## Reviewing Event Proposals

When a department coordinator or committee convener proposes an event, it enters the Principal approval queue:

1. Open the Principal workspace from the navigation menu.
2. Under **Principal Approvals**, select an event proposal to review its complete submission details:
   * **Academic Merit & Relevance:** Objectives, agenda, and organizing committee.
   * **Schedule & Venue:** Dates, timings, venue suitability, and potential campus scheduling conflicts.
   * **Audience & Eligibility:** Target audience classification and participating departments.
   * **Capacity & Resources:** Maximum participant counts and required institutional facilities.
3. Choose an approval decision:
   * **Approve:** Formally approves the proposal. The event transitions to published status, making it visible to eligible staff and students.
   * **Request Changes:** Returns the proposal to the coordinator with specific written remarks detailing required modifications.
   * **Reject:** Rejects the proposal with written justification. The event is closed and will not proceed.

---

## Approving Event Revisions

When an organizer needs to modify details of an already-approved event (such as updating dates, changing venue, or adjusting session schedules):

* **Revision Workflow:** The coordinator submits the requested modifications as a formal revision.
* **Version Stability:** To prevent disruption to participants, the previously approved version remains publicly visible and active until the Principal reviews and approves the new revision.
* **Decision:** Once approved by the Principal, the revised event details immediately supersede the earlier version across the catalog and registration portals.

---

## Reviewing Post-Event Report Packages

After an event concludes, the organizing coordinator compiles and submits an event report package for administrative review:

### Unified Package Evaluation
The Principal reviews the report, selected media, and press note together as one unified submission:
* **Executive Summary:** Overview of event execution and outcomes.
* **Structured Sections:** Highlights covering key sessions, speaker contributions, and student engagement.
* **Attendance Statistics:** Verified participant counts categorized by staff, students, and volunteers.
* **Curated Media:** Up to six high-resolution event photographs with explanatory captions.
* **Press Note:** Formatted institutional press release intended for college archives, annual reports, and media releases.

### Decision Actions
* **Approve Report Package:** Approves the entire package. The event is formally completed and cleared for permanent institutional archival.
* **Request Changes:** Sends the report package back to the coordinator with specific guidance on sections or media captions requiring revision.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Re-Approval Requirement**
If an approved report is subsequently edited by a coordinator, the updated report package must be resubmitted and re-approved by the Principal before the revised contents can be published or archived.
{% endhint %}

---

## Event Cancellation Authority

In the event of unforeseen scheduling conflicts, campus emergencies, or administrative orders:

* The Principal holds the authority to cancel an individual scheduled session or the entire event.
* When cancelling, a formal administrative reason must be recorded.
* Registered participants are immediately notified, and the event listing updates to reflect the cancellation.
