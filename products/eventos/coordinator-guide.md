# Department Coordinator Guide for EventOS

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** Department Coordinators, Committee Conveners, Event Organizers
* **Applies To:** EventOS (Coordinator Workspace)
* **Scope:** Events owned by your assigned campus and department
{% endhint %}

This guide explains how department coordinators plan and propose events, schedule sessions, and prepare for upcoming attendance and reporting features.

---

## Coordinator Workspace Overview

Coordinators access management tools through the **Coordinator Workspace**:

* **My Proposed Events:** Track the status of events you have submitted for approval (Draft, Under Review, Changes Requested, Approved, Published, Completed).
* **Assigned Events:** Events where you are assigned as an organizer or attendance manager.
* **Quick Actions:** Propose a new event or review submitted proposals.

---

## Proposing a New Event

To propose an academic or co-curricular event:

1. Open the Coordinator Workspace and select **Propose Event**.
2. Complete the required event details:
   * **Event Title & Description:** Clear title and summary of objectives and agenda.
   * **Organizing Committee:** Select your primary committee, cell, or department (and optional collaborating committees).
   * **Event Category:** Choose category (e.g., Seminar, Workshop, Conference, Cultural, Sports).
   * **Dates & Schedule:** Specify start and end dates and times, including multi-day schedules.
   * **Venue Selection:** Choose a pre-configured institutional venue on your campus, or specify a custom venue.
3. Configure audience and participant eligibility:
   * **Audience Type:** Select Staff, Students, or Staff and Students.
   * **Campus Scope:** Restrict eligibility to your campus or permit cross-campus enrollment.
   * **Eligible Departments:** Select one, several, or all departments eligible to participate.
4. Set registration parameters:
   * **Registration Window:** Specify the exact opening and closing dates and times.
   * **Capacity Limit:** Enter a maximum participant count, or leave blank for unlimited capacity.
5. Click **Submit for Approval** to forward the proposal to the campus Principal.
