# Department Coordinator Guide for EventOS

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** Department Coordinators, Committee Conveners, Event Organizers
* **Applies To:** EventOS (Coordinator Workspace)
* **Scope:** Events owned by your assigned campus and department
{% endhint %}

This guide explains how department coordinators plan and propose events, schedule sessions, coordinate venue helpers, manage attendance windows, and submit consolidated post-event reports.

---

## Coordinator Workspace Overview

Coordinators access management tools through the **Coordinator Workspace**:

* **My Proposed Events:** Track the status of events you have submitted for approval (Draft, Under Review, Changes Requested, Approved, Published, Completed).
* **Assigned Events:** Events where you are assigned as an organizer or attendance manager.
* **Quick Actions:** Propose a new event, manage session check-ins, or compile post-event reports.

---

## Proposing a New Event

To propose an academic or co-curricular event:

1. Open the Coordinator Workspace and select **Propose Event**.
2. Complete the required event details:
   * **Event Title & Description:** Clear title and summary of objectives and agenda.
   * **Organizing Committee:** Select your primary committee, cell, or department (and optional collaborating committees).
   * **Event Category:** Choose category (e.g., Seminar, Workshop, Conference, Cultural, Sports).
   * **Dates & Schedule:** Specify start and end dates and times, including multi-day schedules.
   * **Venue Selection:** Choose a pre-configured institutional venue on your campus, or specify a custom venue.
3. Configure audience and participant eligibility:
   * **Audience Type:** Select Staff, Students, or Staff and Students.
   * **Campus Scope:** Restrict eligibility to your campus or permit cross-campus enrollment.
   * **Eligible Departments:** Select one, several, or all departments eligible to participate.
4. Set registration parameters:
   * **Registration Window:** Specify the exact opening and closing dates and times.
   * **Capacity Limit:** Enter a maximum participant count, or leave blank for unlimited capacity.
5. Click **Submit for Approval** to forward the proposal to the campus Principal.

## Coordinating Same-Campus Helpers

Coordinators can assign faculty or staff members from the same campus as session helpers:

* **Role of Helpers:** Helpers assist with operational tasks during the event, primarily scanning attendee QR passes at venue entrances.
* **Access Boundary:** Assigned helpers have access restricted strictly to the attendance scanner for that specific session. They cannot modify event details, approve registrations, or edit reports.
* **Campus Restriction:** Helpers must belong to the same campus where the event is hosted.

## Managing Session Attendance

Attendance in EventOS is tracked independently for each session:

### Opening the Attendance Window
* Attendance recording is time-restricted. The coordinator opens the attendance window at the start of each session.
* EventOS displays a static session QR code on the venue screen, or coordinators and helpers scan individual attendee passes.

### Scanning Attendee QR Passes
1. Open **Attendance** in the Coordinator Workspace on a smartphone or tablet.
2. Select the current event session.
3. Point the camera at the participant's digital QR pass.
4. The scanner validates the pass in real time and displays a confirmation checkmark with the participant's name and department.

### Manual Check-In Fallback
If a participant experiences phone battery failure or display issues, the coordinator can search for the participant by name or reference number in the session roster and record attendance manually.

### Extending the Attendance Window
If session start is delayed or entry queues remain, the coordinator can extend the active attendance window directly from the attendance management panel.

## Submitting Post-Event Reports

Once all sessions are completed, the coordinator compiles and submits a consolidated event report package:

### Components of the Report Package
A complete report package consists of four interconnected parts submitted together:
1. **Executive Narrative:** Event summary highlighting key outcomes, speakers, and achievements.
2. **Detailed Sections:** Up to six structured content sections (such as inauguration, keynote highlights, technical sessions, and valedictory remarks).
3. **Participant Statistics:** Verified attendance breakdown (total participants, staff count, student count, and volunteer count).
4. **Photographs & Captions:** Up to six selected high-resolution event photographs, each accompanied by an informative descriptive caption.
5. **Press Note:** A summary of the event for college announcements and records.

### Report Review & Approval
1. Review all sections, statistics, captions, and the press note.
2. Click **Submit Report Package** to send the entire package to the Principal for formal review.
3. If the Principal requests revisions, the coordinator receives specific feedback remarks, updates the requested sections, and resubmits the package for re-approval.
