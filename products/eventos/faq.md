## 1. General and Access

### What is EventOS?
EventOS is the academic and co-curricular event lifecycle management platform for Amroli College. It connects proposal drafting, executive approvals, staff registration, session attendance tracking, reporting, and archival in a single system.

### Who can access EventOS?
EventOS is available to all teaching faculty and non-teaching staff members who possess an active, verified account in StaffBridge. Access is introduced progressively in stages.

### Which login credentials do I use?
Sign in using your college Google Workspace account ending in `@amrolicollege.ac.in` via the JyotOS launcher. Separate passwords are not required.

### How are roles assigned in EventOS?
EventOS roles (Staff, Department Coordinator, Principal, Administrator) are assigned independently by System Administrators in accordance with institutional responsibilities.
→ See [EventOS Overview](overview.md).

## 2. Event Proposals and Approvals

### Who can propose an event?
Authorized Department Coordinators and Committee Conveners can draft and submit event proposals for their assigned campus and department.

### What information is required in an event proposal?
A proposal requires an event title, description, organizing committee, event category, multi-day schedule, campus venue, target audience, department eligibility rules, registration window, and optional capacity limit.

### Who approves event proposals?
Event proposals must be formally reviewed and approved by the campus Principal before they can be published.

### What happens when an approved event is edited?
When an organizer submits changes to an already-approved event, the updates enter a revision review. The previously approved event details remain published and visible to participants until the Principal formally approves the new revision.
→ See [Coordinator Guide](coordinator-guide.md) and [Principal Guide](principal-guide.md).

## 3. Registration and Eligibility

### Do I need to register separately for each session of an event?
No. You register once for the event. Your confirmed registration grants you access to all sessions within that event.

### Why do some events state I am not eligible?
Events can specify department-level or campus-level eligibility. If your profile is not mapped to an eligible department or campus, registration is blocked.

### Can I join an event that has reached full capacity?
If a waiting list is active, you can join the waiting list. If an enrolled participant cancels, waitlisted applicants are automatically promoted on a first-come, first-served basis.

### When does registration close?
Registration closes automatically at the date and time specified by the event organizer. Once closed, new registrations cannot be accepted.

### Can I cancel my registration?
Yes, you can cancel your registration from the My Registrations page, but only while the event registration window remains open. Cancellations are disabled once registration closes or attendance has been marked.
→ See [Staff Guide](staff-guide.md).
