This guide helps resolve technical difficulties encountered while capturing passport photos or drawing signatures in StaffBridge.

***

## Media Troubleshooting Decision Table

| What You Experience               | Likely Cause                              | What To Do                                                                               |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Camera View Black / Disabled**  | Browser camera permission blocked         | Open browser site settings, set **Camera** permission to **Allow**, and reload page.     |
| **Photo Upload Fails**            | File size too large or unsupported format | Ensure image is in JPEG/PNG format and under recommended file size.                      |
| **Signature Canvas Unresponsive** | Touch screen or mouse drawing outside box | Click **Clear Canvas** and draw your signature smoothly inside the white box boundaries. |
| **Uploaded Signature Blurry**     | Shadow or pale ink in scanned image       | Use dark blue or black ink on unlined white paper in bright lighting.                    |
