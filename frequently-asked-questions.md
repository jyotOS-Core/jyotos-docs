## 1. Getting Started & Access

### What is StaffBridge?

StaffBridge is the official staff identity, onboarding, profile management, and verification platform of Amroli College. It establishes a single trusted system of record for all academic faculty and administrative staff across college campuses.

### Who should complete StaffBridge onboarding?

All newly appointed and existing faculty members, administrative officers, laboratory assistants, library personnel, and institutional support staff must complete StaffBridge onboarding.

### Do I need an account before completing onboarding?

No. Initial onboarding registration at `/staff/onboarding` is publicly accessible and does not require prior login. After submitting registration, you log into your dashboard using your official Google `@amrolicollege.ac.in` email account.

### Which email address should I use for StaffBridge?

You must use your official college email address ending strictly with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.

### Can I use a personal Gmail or Yahoo account?

No. StaffBridge authentication requires an official `@amrolicollege.ac.in` Google account. Personal email domains are restricted by institutional access policies.\
→ _Detailed Guide:_ Can't Access StaffBridge

### What is my Staff Reference ID and where can I find it?

Your Staff Reference ID is a unique institutional tracking code generated upon registration. It is displayed on your submission confirmation page, PDF receipt, and Staff Dashboard header.

### What happens after I submit my onboarding form?

Your submission enters your department's verification queue under status **Submitted**. Your assigned Department Coordinator evaluates your details before advancing your profile to Coordinator Verification.\

## 2. Onboarding & Information

### Can I edit my information after submitting onboarding?

Once submitted, profile fields become locked to prevent unauthorized changes during review. If a field requires updating, your Department Coordinator or System Administrator can issue a correction request to unlock specific fields for editing.

### What should I do if I enter incorrect information?

If you realize you entered incorrect information after submitting, notify your assigned Department Coordinator. They can flag the affected field for correction on your dashboard.

### Why can't I select my primary department?

Ensure you select your **Primary Campus** (**Amroli Campus** or **Chaprabhatha Campus**) first. The department dropdown dynamically populates based on the chosen campus.

### Can I select more than one primary department?

StaffBridge requires selecting one primary academic or administrative department during onboarding. _(Note: Guidance for formal multi-department joint appointments requires Product Owner confirmation)._

### What should I do if StaffBridge says my registration is a duplicate?

If a notice indicates a duplicate registration under your email or mobile number, do not attempt to register again. Log into your Staff Dashboard to view your existing profile or contact your System Administrator.

### What information should I prepare before onboarding?

Prepare your personal details, residential address with pincode, emergency contact information, official appointment designation, date of joining, academic qualification list, passport photo, and signature image.\
→ _Detailed Guide:_ Complete Your Staff Onboarding

## 3. Photo & Signature Media

### Are my photograph and signature mandatory?

Yes. Uploading a clear passport-style photo and signature image is mandatory to complete onboarding and generate your Digital ID preview.

### Can I upload an existing photograph file?

Yes. You can upload an existing image file (JPEG or PNG format) or use your camera to take a live photo.

### Can I use my mobile device camera for live photo capture?

Yes. When prompted by your mobile browser, grant camera permissions to capture your photo directly within StaffBridge.

### What type of photograph is recommended?

Use a clear, front-facing portrait photo taken against a plain light background in well-lit conditions, without sunglasses or hats.

### Can I clear and redraw my signature?

Yes. You can tap or click **Clear Canvas** to erase your drawing and redraw your signature as many times as needed before saving.

### Who can view my uploaded photograph and signature?

Your media is accessible only to authorized Department Coordinators, System Administrators, and the College Principal for verification and ID generation purposes.\
→ _Detailed Guide:_ Photo & Signature Guidelines

## 4. Profile Status & Verification

### What does each Profile Status mean?

* **Submitted:** Form received; in queue for department review.
* **Coordinator Verified:** Department Coordinator verified credentials and department mapping.
* **Needs Correction:** Reviewer requested an update to one or more fields.
* **Correction Submitted:** Updated details submitted by staff; awaiting re-evaluation.
* **Admin Verified:** Final institutional approval granted by Administrator or Principal.
* **Duplicate / Rejected:** Flagged as duplicate or unusable by administration.

### How long does the verification process take?

Verification time depends entirely on institutional review schedules and department verification volume. StaffBridge does not enforce automated approval SLAs.

### What is the difference between Profile Status and Active Status?

* **Profile Status:** Measures review lifecycle progress (**Submitted** ──► **Coordinator Verified** ──► **Admin Verified**).
* **Active Status:** Manages operational access enablement (**Active** / **Inactive**).

### Who manages Active Status?

Active Status is managed exclusively by System Administrators and the College Principal.

### Why might a verified profile still show access restrictions?

A profile may be **Admin Verified** but set to **Inactive** by an administrator. Contact your System Administrator to request Active Status enablement.\
→ _Detailed Guide:_ Understanding Your Profile Status

## 5. Correction Requests

### Why was my profile returned for correction?

A reviewer flagged a specific field that was incomplete, formatted incorrectly, or contained low-quality media (e.g., address missing pincode or blurry signature).

### How do I know which fields need correction?

Log into your Staff Dashboard. Flagged fields are highlighted under the **Pending Correction Fields** section alongside specific reviewer remarks.

### Can I update fields that were not flagged for correction?

No. Unflagged fields remain locked to preserve verified data integrity.

### What should I do if I don't understand a reviewer remark?

Review the guidance in Responding to Correction Requests. If still unclear, contact your Department Coordinator directly for clarification.

### What happens after I resubmit corrected information?

Your status changes to **Correction Submitted**, notifying your coordinator to re-evaluate the updated details.\
→ _Detailed Guide:_ Responding to Correction Requests

## 6. Staff Dashboard & Digital ID

### What can I see on my Staff Dashboard?

Your dashboard displays your current Profile Status, Staff Reference ID, personal profile summary, pending correction alerts, and Digital ID card preview.

### Where can I check my Profile Status?

Your Profile Status is prominently displayed in the status badge card at the top of your Staff Dashboard.

### What is the Digital ID preview on my dashboard?

The Digital ID preview provides an on-screen preview of your verified staff identity card including your photo, name, designation, department, and Staff Reference ID.

### Why isn't my Digital ID information appearing correctly?

The Digital ID preview renders fully after mandatory media fields (photo and signature) are uploaded and submitted.\
→ _Detailed Guide:_ Using Your Staff Dashboard

## 7. Department Coordinators

### Which staff records can a Department Coordinator see?

Coordinators can view onboarding submissions, profile sheets, media, and correction responses for personnel assigned to their primary or additional departments.

### Can a Department Coordinator review staff from another department?

No. Access boundaries restrict coordinators to their assigned academic or administrative units.

### When should a Coordinator request a correction versus granting verification?

Request a correction if any field is inaccurate, incomplete, or contains low-quality media. Grant Coordinator Verification only when all details are confirmed accurate against college records.

### What happens after a Coordinator grants verification?

The profile advances to **Coordinator Verified** status and enters the System Administrator queue for final institutional approval.\
→ _Detailed Guide:_ Department Coordinator Guide

***

## 8. System Administrators

### Who can perform final verification?

Final administrative verification (**Admin Verified**) is performed exclusively by System Administrators and the College Principal.

### Who can enable or disable Active Status?

System Administrators and the College Principal manage staff Active Status (**Active** / **Inactive**).

### Who can access institution-wide Staff Reports?

System Administrators and the College Principal access the **Staff Reports & Verification Roster** page.

### Can staff reports be filtered and exported?

Yes. Reports can be filtered by campus, department, submission status, and active status, and exported as a downloadable CSV spreadsheet.\
→ _Detailed Guide:_ System Administrator Guide

## 9. Data Governance & Privacy

### Why does StaffBridge collect staff information?

StaffBridge collects staff identity, employment, academic, and contact details to maintain a single trusted institutional roster for academic administration and identity management.

### Who has permission to review my submitted information?

Submitted records are accessible only to authorized Department Coordinators (department scope), System Administrators, and the College Principal.

### How are uploaded photos and signatures used?

Photos and signatures are used strictly for staff identity verification, digital ID card generation, and institutional records management.

### Is my data shared with third parties?

StaffBridge identity data is managed internally within Amroli College systems and is not shared with external third-party commercial vendors. _(Note: Formal institutional privacy policies remain subject to Product Owner confirmation)._

## 10. JyotOS Ecosystem Access

### How does StaffBridge connect to the broader JyotOS ecosystem?

StaffBridge serves as the central identity provider for Amroli College. Verified staff identities form the foundation for access across connected JyotOS applications (such as EventOS).

### Will I need to re-enter my staff information in every JyotOS application?

No. Connected JyotOS applications reference your verified StaffBridge profile, eliminating duplicate data entry.

### Does StaffBridge automatically grant access to all JyotOS applications?

StaffBridge establishes **ecosystem access eligibility** for verified active staff. Specific application permissions and role assignments are managed by application administrators. _(Note: Specific SSO authentication mechanisms remain subject to Product Owner confirmation)._\
