## General Platform Questions

### What is JyotOS?
JyotOS is the unified institutional operating system developed for Amroli College. It connects identity, administrative operations, communication, and event management under a single platform.

### What products are currently part of JyotOS?
* **Central:** The centralized administrative hub for system navigation, role-based application launch, and institutional governance.
* **StaffBridge:** The central identity authority and onboarding platform for all faculty and administrative staff.
* **EventOS:** The institutional event scheduling and management system.

### How does Single Sign-On (SSO) work in JyotOS?
StaffBridge serves as the central identity authority. When a staff member completes onboarding and their profile reaches **Ready** status (`admin_verified` with Active Status set to `Active`), their identity is recognized across connected JyotOS applications without duplicate registration.

### Which email address should I use across JyotOS applications?
All JyotOS applications require sign-in using your official Google Workspace account ending with `@amrolicollege.ac.in`. Personal email accounts (such as `@gmail.com`) are not accepted.

## Roles and Access

### How are user roles determined across JyotOS?
User roles are governed through StaffBridge Access Control (`/admin/access-control`) and Central access settings. Roles include `staff`, `coordinator`, `principal`, and `admin`.

### Who do I contact if my account is inactive?
If your account displays an inactive notice, contact your System Administrator to verify your employment status and activate your record.
