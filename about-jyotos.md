Welcome to **JyotOS** — the integrated institutional management ecosystem for Amroli College.

JyotOS brings together specialized software applications into a single, cohesive framework designed to support academic administration, staff governance, student services, and institutional operations.

***

## Why JyotOS Contains Multiple Applications

Rather than relying on a single complex system, JyotOS is built with specialized applications designed for specific institutional functions:

* **Focused User Experience:** Each application provides a simple, dedicated interface tailored to specific tasks—such as staff onboarding, event management, or academic rosters.
* **Collaborative Operations:** Applications work seamlessly together behind the scenes, sharing verified identity records so users do not need to re-enter information across different tools.
* **Institutional Governance:** Unified access standards ensure that staff members, coordinators, principals, and administrators maintain appropriate access rights across all campus services.

***

## StaffBridge: The Identity Foundation

**StaffBridge** is the first documented application within the JyotOS ecosystem.

It serves as the official identity foundation, capturing staff onboarding details, facilitating department verification, and providing trusted identity information to supported institutional applications.
