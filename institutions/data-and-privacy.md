## Staff Information Collected by StaffBridge

StaffBridge serves as the institutional directory of verified staff profiles. During the self-service onboarding process, the following information fields are collected:

### 1. Personal & Contact Details
* Full legal name
* Official college email address (`@amrolicollege.ac.in`)
* Primary mobile contact number
* Emergency contact information
* Date of birth and blood group

### 2. Employment & Institutional Mapping
* Primary institutional designation
* Assigned academic or administrative department
* Employment classification (Teaching or Non-Teaching cadre)
* Official joining date
* Campus location assignment (Amroli Campus or Chaprabhatha Campus)

### 3. Academic & Professional Qualifications
* Educational degrees and certificates
* Disciplines and specializations
* Awarding universities, boards, or institutions
* Year of completion

### 4. Digital Identification Media
* Formal passport-style photograph for digital ID generation
* Digital signature specimen for official documentation
* Formal user declaration confirming information accuracy

## Application Scoping & Role Ownership

Within the JyotOS ecosystem:

* **Central Access Control:** JyotOS Central determines which application tiles appear on a user's dashboard based on administrator provisioning.
* **Independent Application Roles:** Each connected application maintains its own domain-specific roles and workflows. For example:
  * EventOS manages event coordinators, reviewers, and participant lists.
  * ClassPulse Pro manages faculty lecture allocations, teaching diaries, and departmental oversight.
* **Departmental Scoping:** Department coordinators and academic leaders can only view and review staff records and academic rosters belonging to their authorized department.
