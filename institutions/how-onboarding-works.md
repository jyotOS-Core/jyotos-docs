# Institutional Onboarding Workflow

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quick Facts**
* **Audience:** College Management, Department Coordinators, System Administrators
* **Applies To:** Staff Onboarding & System Adoption

This page outlines the onboarding sequence for faculty and staff joining the JyotOS ecosystem, derived directly from product workflows in StaffBridge and JyotOS Central.

---

## The 4-Stage Onboarding Process

```
[Stage 1: Sign In] → [Stage 2: Profile Submission] → [Stage 3: Verification] → [Stage 4: App Access]
```

### Stage 1: Official Account Sign In
* Faculty and staff sign in using their official institutional Google account ending in `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.
* Single sign-on authentication verifies the user's institutional domain without requiring separate passwords for connected services.

### Stage 2: Profile Submission in StaffBridge
Upon first login, staff members are directed to StaffBridge to submit their institutional profile:
* **Personal Information:** Full name, contact email, mobile number, emergency contact, date of birth, and blood group.
* **Employment Classification:** Designation, department mapping, employment type (Teaching / Non-Teaching), and joining date.
* **Qualifications:** Educational degrees, specializations, passing years, and awarding universities.
* **Campus Selection:** Primary campus assignment (Amroli Campus or Chaprabhatha Campus).
* **Media Uploads:** Formal passport-style photograph and clear digital signature specimen.
* **Staff Declaration:** Formal confirmation of the submitted record accuracy.

### Stage 3: Two-Tier Profile Verification
Submissions progress through a two-stage institutional review:
1. **Department Coordinator Review:** Department coordinators examine submitted details, confirm academic credentials, and verify media suitability. Coordinators can approve the profile or request corrections with specific notes.
2. **Administrator Verification:** System administrators conduct final checks, assign an official Staff ID number, and mark the profile as **Admin Verified** (Ready profile).

### Stage 4: Central Application Access Provisioning
Once a staff profile is verified:
* **Default Access:** Standard self-service tools appear on the staff member's JyotOS Central dashboard.
* **Role-Based Tools:** Administrators assign specialized application access (such as EventOS proposal tools or ClassPulse Pro academic workspaces) directly to individuals or target audiences.
* **Phased Rollout:** Institutions typically begin with pilot staff groups before expanding access across all departments.
