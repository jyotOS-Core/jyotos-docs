Welcome to **JyotOS**, the integrated digital campus platform for Amroli Campus and Chaprabhatha Campus.

---

## 1. How to Sign In

* **Website:** Open the JyotOS Central portal in your web browser.
* **Account:** Sign in using your official college Google account ending in `@amrolicollege.ac.in`.
* **No Separate Password:** Sign in once with Google to reach your authorized campus applications.

---

## 2. Your 4 Live Applications

| Application | What It Does for You | How to Start |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **JyotOS Central** | Your central dashboard. Displays your authorized tools, appearance settings, and access request buttons. | Open directly after sign-in. Click any active tile to launch the tool. |
| **StaffBridge** | Your official staff profile and digital ID. Submit your details, upload your photo and signature, and track review status. | Open from Central. Complete onboarding if you are a new staff member. |
| **EventOS** | Campus event portal. Explore upcoming academic seminars, workshops, and college programs, and register online. | Open from Central. Browse the active event catalog and enroll. |
| **ClassPulse Pro** | Faculty academic workspace. View your assigned timetable slots, track lecture delivery, and maintain your teaching diary. | Access from Central or designated portal using your pilot login. |

---

## 3. Profile Onboarding Checklist

When completing your profile in StaffBridge, ensure you have:
1. Contact details and emergency contact information
2. Employment designation and department assignment
3. Academic qualification records
4. Formal passport-style photo (JPEG or PNG format)
5. Clear digital signature image

---

## 4. Need Help?

* **Department Inquiries:** Contact your Department Coordinator or Head of Department (HOD) for profile review questions.
* **Technical Assistance:** Contact the **College IT Cell** or your **System Administrator** for login issues or access permissions.
* **Documentation:** Visit the online help pages at [Help Center](../help/troubleshooting.md).
